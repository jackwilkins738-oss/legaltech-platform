# CLINICAL NEGLIGENCE — PUBLIC-SOURCE WORKFLOW MODEL

## Core protocol stages

### 1. Intake
- client
- treatment/provider
- dates
- alleged injury
- limitation
- initial chronology
- funding

### 2. Health records
Protocol specifically provides for obtaining relevant health records.

Automation opportunities:
- provider list
- record request drafting
- tracking
- chase
- completeness check
- duplicate detection
- indexing
- OCR/chronology assistance

### 3. Initial investigation
- chronology
- identify potential breach
- causation
- prognosis
- evidence gaps

AI may assist:
- chronology
- fact extraction
- missing records

AI must not decide:
- negligence
- causation
- merits

### 4. Expert evidence
Protocol recognises separate opinions may be required for:
- breach
- causation
- condition/prognosis
- valuation

Automation:
- expert instruction packs
- document sets
- chase
- version tracking
- question/response tracking

Human:
- expert selection
- legal analysis
- reliance on reports

### 5. Letter of Notification / Claim
- prepare
- supporting chronology
- allegations
- injury/prognosis
- records index

Automation:
- populate factual sections
- assemble evidence
- completeness review

Human:
- approve allegations/legal content

### 6. Defendant response
Protocol gives defendant time to investigate/respond.

Automation:
- deadline
- acknowledgement
- response classification
- admission/denial extraction
- action queue

### 7. ADR / negotiation
Human-led legal judgement.

### 8. Proceedings
Same operational opportunities as complex litigation:
- deadline extraction
- bundle preparation
- disclosure workflow
- expert management
- chronology
- correspondence

## Why later than PI
Advantages:
- enormous document burden
- records complexity
- expert coordination

Risks:
- extensive health data
- nuanced expert/legal judgement
- larger consequences from incorrect inference
- lower standardisation in complex matters
