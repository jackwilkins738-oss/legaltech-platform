# ROLE INTERVIEW GUIDES

## Every role
1. What do you do most often in Proclaim?
2. What feels like it should be automatic?
3. What do you do outside Proclaim?
4. What do you copy/paste repeatedly?
5. What do you manually check?
6. What do you chase?
7. What creates duplicate work?
8. What regularly goes wrong?
9. What is hardest to train?
10. What would you never trust automation to do?
11. What does Proclaim do very well?
12. If one task disappeared tomorrow, which saves most time?

## Legal admin focus
Filing, email, scanning/upload, naming, letters, chasing, data entry, matter opening, client updates.

## Case manager / fee earner focus
Prioritisation, evidence, deadlines, review, drafting, supervision, missing information, delegation.

## Supervisor focus
Workload, overdue work, risk, supervision, reporting, capacity.

## IT focus
Installs, upgrades, tickets, outages, Outlook/Office, integrations, performance, permissions, reporting/database, backup/DR, vendor support.

## Management focus
Cost, utilisation, risk, profitability, support spend, switching concerns, procurement/security, automation appetite.
