# OPPORTUNITY SCORING MODEL

Score 1–5.

## Value
Frequency, staff affected, time, frustration, error/rework, delay, financial value, buyer visibility.

## Automation feasibility
Rule determinism, AI feasibility, data availability, integration feasibility, reversibility, measurability.

## Risk (higher = worse)
Legal judgement dependency, confidentiality, deadline, financial impact, external action, hallucination impact.

## Commercial
Willingness to pay, ease of pilot, ROI proof, deployment friction, expansion potential, differentiation.

## Candidates to score
Email-to-matter filing; document classification; task creation from correspondence; chasing; missing-information monitoring; deadline extraction/monitoring; correspondence drafting; matter summaries; management dashboards; time capture; client intake.
