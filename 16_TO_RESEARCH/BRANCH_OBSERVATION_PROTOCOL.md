# FIVE-DAY BRANCH OBSERVATION PROTOCOL

## Objective
Measure what staff actually do, not what they remember doing.

## Roles
Legal admin, case manager, paralegal, solicitor/fee earner, supervisor/team leader, accounts, operations manager, IT/support.

## Record for every task
Timestamp, role, matter type, trigger, task, systems, exact actions, clicks/interactions, context switches, handling time, waiting time, rework, error, approval, output, frequency, why a human was needed, rule-based potential, AI-assist potential, AI-automation potential, risk-if-wrong, frustration score.

## Daily target
- 50 task observations/day where practical
- 10 repetitive admin examples
- 5 waiting/chasing examples
- 5 rework/error examples
- 5 system-switch examples
- 3 examples where Proclaim works well

## Privacy
Do not collect unnecessary client-identifiable content, passwords or secrets.

## Daily review
Merge duplicate task types, calculate handling time, identify high-frequency processes, record exceptions and unknowns.
