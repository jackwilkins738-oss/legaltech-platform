# PAIN ECONOMICS MODEL

## Core calculation
Annual labour hours = occurrences/day × handling minutes × working days × employees affected ÷ 60.

Annual labour cost = annual labour hours × loaded hourly cost.

## Add where relevant
Rework, errors, supervisor review, external supplier cost, IT/support time, lost billable capacity, delay cost, missed-SLA risk, complaints/compliance exposure.

## Automation scenario
Capture automatable %, human-review %, new handling time, annual hours saved and capacity value.

## Important wording
Do not present capacity saved as cash saved unless the firm can genuinely reduce cost. Use 'capacity value' where appropriate.
