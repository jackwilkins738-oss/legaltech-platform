# WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN WITHOUT THE TO STUDY

## Important
At this point the majority of the non-TO business/product/security architecture can be scoped.

The following should NOT be falsely presented as known.

## 1. Exact task frequencies
We do not yet know:
- emails/user/day
- documents/user/day
- chases/day
- manual filing events
in the actual target branch.

## 2. Exact handling times
We do not know:
- average filing time
- task-creation time
- Proclaim navigation time
- context-switch time

## 3. Click counts
Needed to quantify UX friction precisely.

## 4. Actual exception rate
We do not know what % of inbound work is:
- obvious
- ambiguous
- legally complex
- restricted/conflicted

## 5. Real automation ceiling
A workflow may look automatable but contain hidden human judgement.

## 6. Error/rework
Need observed:
- misfiling
- duplicate work
- missed follow-up
- incorrect fields
- failed Proclaim actions

## 7. True economic value
ROI models are assumptions until:
frequency × time × staff × real loaded cost
is measured.

## 8. Final wedge ranking
Inbound email/document remains strongest hypothesis.
TO can still disprove it.

## 9. User adoption
We do not know:
- review behavior
- trust
- resistance
- workarounds
until tested.

## 10. Firm-specific Proclaim setup
Need access to know:
- fields
- workflows
- APIs
- modules
- customisations
- integrations
- permissions

## 11. Production API behavior
Community/public evidence is not substitute for test environment.

## 12. Willingness to pay
Cannot be completed from desk research.

## What is sufficiently scoped before TO
- market thesis
- competitor threat
- ICP hypothesis
- business model
- architecture
- security
- privacy
- AI governance
- Graph integration
- Proclaim connector design
- data model
- workflow engine
- exception UX
- migration strategy
- procurement
- certifications
- support
- contracts
- scale strategy

## Conclusion
The project should now pause broad speculative discovery and:
1. consolidate the research
2. run the TO study
3. validate actual Proclaim integration
4. interview external firms
5. choose the first wedge using evidence
