# Transferability and standardisation

The first firm's pain is not automatically a market.

For each top workflow classify:
- legal/protocol-driven vs firm-policy-driven;
- practice-specific vs broadly legal;
- role-specific vs universal;
- standard steps vs bespoke judgement;
- dependence on local Proclaim configuration;
- dependence on a specific insurer/provider/court;
- likely transferability to another firm.

## Opportunity test
A strong software wedge is:
- painful;
- frequent or high-consequence;
- safe enough to standardise;
- data-accessible;
- economically meaningful;
- repeatable across more than one firm.

Do not let a highly customised one-firm workaround become the product architecture.
