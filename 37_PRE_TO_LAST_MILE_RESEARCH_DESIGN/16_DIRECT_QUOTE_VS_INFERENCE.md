# Direct quote vs observer inference

Qualitative evidence should preserve the distinction between what the worker said and what the observer concluded.

## Store separately
**Direct worker explanation (sanitised):**
> “I put this on my own spreadsheet because the report does not show X.”

**Observer inference:**
> Possible reporting/configuration gap; validate with Proclaim admin.

Do not rewrite the inference as if the worker said it.

## Follow-up
Any conclusion about product capability should be validated against:
- Proclaim admin/IT;
- current configuration;
- system evidence;
- current product documentation where relevant.
