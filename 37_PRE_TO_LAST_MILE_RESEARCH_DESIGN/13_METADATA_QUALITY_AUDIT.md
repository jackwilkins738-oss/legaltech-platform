# Metadata-quality audit

A workflow may look ideal for automation but fail because the underlying matter metadata is missing, inconsistent or stale.

Where authorised, take a small metadata-only sample and inspect fields automation would depend on:
- matter reference;
- case type;
- stage;
- owner/team;
- open/closed state;
- task status;
- history/document type;
- dates;
- external-party type;
- structured identifiers.

Classify issues:
- missing;
- inconsistent;
- free-text only;
- duplicate;
- stale;
- wrong stage;
- wrong owner.

Do not copy document content merely to perform the metadata audit.

Poor data quality is not a reason to hide the opportunity; it is a prerequisite/cost that must be included in the product decision.
