# Participant transparency and DPIA decision

## Before observation
Confirm the firm has decided and documented:
- purpose of observation;
- authority to conduct it;
- what worker data will be captured;
- lawful basis/appropriate privacy handling;
- whether a DPIA is required and, if not, why not;
- retention/deletion period;
- who can access raw notes;
- how workers are informed.

## Worker briefing
Workers should understand:
- this is process/workflow research, not individual performance ranking;
- no covert audio/video/screenshot/keystroke recording is being used;
- no client-identifiable content should be copied into the study;
- analysis is role/process level where possible.

## Important
ICO guidance treats occasional short-term monitoring as worker monitoring too. Transparency, necessity, proportionality, data minimisation and retention still matter.
