# Work-sampling fallback

Continuous observation is preferred for sequence, interruptions and task duration, but it may become impractical if the observer cannot keep up.

## Fallback
Use a predefined work-sampling schedule rather than convenience observation:
- choose an interval before the session;
- record the worker's current activity at each sampling point;
- keep the interval rule fixed;
- record missed samples.

## What it can estimate
Work sampling can help estimate the proportion of observed time spent in broad activity classes.

## What it cannot replace
It does not provide:
- precise individual-task durations;
- full workflow sequence;
- detailed handoffs;
- reliable exception-path timing.

Do not mix work-sampling prevalence estimates with targeted-repeat timings without labelling the evidence source.
