# Research basis

This last-mile layer is based on established workflow-study and process-mining principles plus current UK worker-monitoring guidance.

## Workflow observation
Time-motion/workflow research literature highlights:
- continuous observation as a way to capture fragmentation, interruptions and unexpected work;
- observer burden and reliability as a substantive methodological risk;
- task coding, duration and sequence as separate reliability dimensions;
- second-level timestamping in workflow studies.

Reference:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3900222/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7153105/

## Process mining
Traditional event logs require at minimum:
- case/process instance;
- activity;
- timestamp.

Lifecycle/resource/object attributes add richer analysis.

Reference:
https://processmining.org/event-data.html

## Worker monitoring
Current ICO guidance states that both systematic and occasional monitoring of workers fall within its guidance. It emphasises necessity, fairness, transparency, minimisation, retention, accountability and DPIA consideration.

Reference:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/monitoring-workers/
