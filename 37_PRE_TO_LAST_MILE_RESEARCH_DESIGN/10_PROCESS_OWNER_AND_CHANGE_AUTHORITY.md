# Process owner and change-authority map

For each top workflow identify:

- operational owner;
- process/SOP owner;
- Proclaim configuration owner;
- IT/integration owner;
- legal/compliance approver;
- data owner;
- who can approve a workflow change;
- who can approve a pilot;
- who can approve external communication automation.

## Why
A workflow can be technically automatable but commercially impossible to change if no owner will approve it.

Conversely, a painful process with a strong owner and short decision path can be an excellent first pilot.

Do not confuse the daily user with the process owner or economic buyer.
