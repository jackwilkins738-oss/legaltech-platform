# Observer-blind protocol

## Why
The observer already knows several product hypotheses: inbound correspondence, matter matching, chasing, exception queues and matter progression. This creates a risk of selectively noticing evidence that supports those hypotheses.

## Rule
During live observation, use only:
- neutral task taxonomy;
- timing/boundary rules;
- anonymisation/privacy rules;
- neutral post-task prompts.

Do **not** keep the product roadmap, competitor map, wedge ranking or pre-registered hypotheses open beside the live capture sheet.

## Neutral prompts
- What told you this needed doing?
- How did you know what to do next?
- Does it always happen this way?
- What happens if this is late or missed?
- Could Proclaim already do any of this differently?
- Why is it manual today?
- What is the cheapest realistic way you would fix it?
- What work did I not see during this session?

## Prohibited prompts
Avoid questions that suggest the desired answer, such as:
- “Would AI help here?”
- “Is Proclaim the problem?”
- “Do you waste lots of time on email?”
- “Would automatic chasing solve this?”

## Debrief separation
Record the observation first. Compare it with hypotheses only during the post-session debrief.
