# POST-OBSERVATION DECISION MEETING — 30 MINUTES

## 1. What was actually observed? — 5m
No opinions first.
- number of events
- roles
- time observed
- practice areas

## 2. Top workload — 5m
- active minutes
- frequency
- repeated workflows

## 3. Surprises / contradictions — 5m
Especially:
- Proclaim already automates something we assumed manual
- different workflow dominates
- work is much more judgement-heavy
- external waiting matters more than admin time

## 4. Provisional top 3 — 5m
Rank using scorecard.

## 5. What evidence is missing? — 5m
Define exactly what tomorrow/next observation must capture.

## 6. Decisions — 5m
Possible:
- CONTINUE inbound hypothesis
- REORDER wedge
- EXPAND observation
- TECHNICAL TEST required
- EXTERNAL VALIDATION required

## Output
Update:
- decision log
- assumptions register
- opportunity scorecard
- next observation plan.
