# ROLE COST ASSUMPTIONS

## Purpose
Convert observed minutes into approximate annual capacity cost.

## Important
The workbook contains **provisional assumptions only** until the firm supplies actual loaded employment-cost data.

Loaded hourly cost can include:
- salary
- employer taxes
- pension/benefits
- other employment cost

It is not the employee's wage.

## Rule
Use the same assumptions consistently during early ranking, then replace with customer finance/HR data before claiming ROI.

## Do not
Present provisional capacity value as cash saving.

Capacity value only becomes cash saving if it:
- avoids a hire;
- removes overtime/contractor spend;
- allows measurable additional case volume;
- produces another demonstrable financial result.
