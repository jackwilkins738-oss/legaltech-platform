# V32 Operations Cockpit

Status: CURRENT SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT SURFACE

V32 upgrades the visible local UI without changing the evidence-gated product scope.

## Added
- My Work is now an operations cockpit.
- Priority work queue orders synthetic operational exceptions by deterministic safety/state rules.
- Priority labels are explicitly not legal-risk or case-value judgements.
- Matter Operations cards show whether a matter is CLEAR, WAITING, IN PROGRESS or BLOCKED.
- Matter detail exposes an operational-state explanation alongside related inbound events and the audit timeline.
- Navigation from My Work to Exception Review and Matter detail is direct.

## Boundaries
- No real client data.
- No production auth.
- No validated legal workflow scoring.
- No final SLA/deadline logic.
- No real Graph or Proclaim behaviour is claimed.
- Commercial workflow selection remains gated by TO/customer evidence.
