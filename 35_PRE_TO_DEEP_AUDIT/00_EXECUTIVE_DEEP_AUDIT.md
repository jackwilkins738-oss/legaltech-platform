# PRE-TO DEEP AUDIT — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This pass asks a harder question than “are we ready to observe?”:

**Can the TO distinguish a genuine software opportunity from configuration debt, training/adoption failure, internal change-capacity limits, policy, data-quality problems, external delay, legal judgement, or a distorted sample?**

The previous pack was strong on task timing and annualisation. The remaining material gaps were:
1. sampling-frame coverage across time/day/stage/role;
2. observer/Hawthorne and concurrency handling;
3. system-derived volume triangulation;
4. Proclaim implementation maturity/configuration debt;
5. module/licence/feature under-use;
6. training/adoption maturity;
7. change backlog/support friction;
8. shadow systems/workarounds;
9. process variants/handoffs;
10. rare/high-impact work that continuous observation may miss;
11. baseline operational KPIs beyond labour minutes;
12. capacity-to-cash realisation evidence;
13. technical environment/integration access;
14. explicit positive controls for what Proclaim does well;
15. study limitations and representativeness.

These are now covered in this folder and the Deep Audit Supplement workbook.
