# CHANGE BACKLOG / SUPPORT FRICTION AUDIT

For each meaningful requested improvement:
- first raised;
- requester;
- current state;
- native capability yes/partial/no/unknown;
- reason unresolved;
- internal effort;
- external consultant/Access cost;
- elapsed lead time;
- operational consequence.

This can reveal a valuable product wedge even when Proclaim technically supports the feature: **time-to-change and maintainability** may be the real problem.
