# RARE / HIGH-IMPACT EVENT METHOD

Continuous observation will under-sample rare events.

Create a separate register for:
- deadline near-misses;
- incorrect matter filing;
- confidentiality incidents;
- duplicated/omitted actions;
- serious client complaint escalation;
- missed evidence/court direction;
- settlement/offer handling failures;
- costly rework.

Use incident/support/interview evidence rather than pretending observed frequency represents annual incidence. Keep probability and consequence separate.
