# BASELINE KPI DICTIONARY

Do not limit baseline to minutes saved.

Track where evidence exists:
- active handling time;
- review burden;
- touches per matter;
- system switches;
- rework rate;
- exception rate;
- queue age/backlog;
- stage/matter cycle time;
- external wait time;
- client status contacts;
- complaint themes/rate;
- matters per handler;
- throughput.

A future pilot should be judged against the subset most directly affected by the selected wedge.
