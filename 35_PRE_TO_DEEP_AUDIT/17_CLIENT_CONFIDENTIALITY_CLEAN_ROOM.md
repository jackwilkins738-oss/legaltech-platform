# CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY / CLEAN-ROOM PROTOCOL

SRA confidentiality duties apply to current/former client affairs and extend beyond simply avoiding disclosure. Do not copy privileged/client-identifiable content into general research notes or unapproved AI tools.

Preferred evidence:
- generic task descriptions;
- anonymised matter aliases;
- counts/timestamps/metadata where authorised;
- synthetic examples for technical testing.

If a representative document is genuinely required later, use a firm-approved data-processing/storage route and minimum necessary content.

Sources:
- https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/confidentiality-client-information/
- https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/misuse-ai
