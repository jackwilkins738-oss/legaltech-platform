# CAPACITY → CASH / VALUE REALISATION

Released hours can become value through:
1. avoided hire;
2. reduced overtime/temps;
3. increased case throughput;
4. faster settlement/billing/cash conversion;
5. redeployment to higher-value legal work;
6. lower complaint/rework/risk cost.

For every ROI claim label which route applies and whether evidence exists.

If there is no route, call it capacity, not saving.
