# SHADOW SYSTEMS / WORKAROUNDS

- Excel/Sheets;
- Outlook folders, flags, drafts;
- personal reminders;
- Word templates outside Proclaim;
- Teams/chat;
- shared drives/desktops;
- whiteboards/manual trackers;
- macros/Power Automate/scripts;
- copied data between systems.

For each: purpose, role, frequency, why Proclaim is not used, risk, rework, and whether it represents missing capability or adoption/config debt.
