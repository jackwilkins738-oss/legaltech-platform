# FINAL PRE-TO GO / NO-GO

## GO if
- firm authorisation exists;
- workers understand observation purpose as appropriate;
- anonymisation rules understood;
- active TO workbook available;
- observer calibrated;
- sampling frame/limitations can be recorded;
- no need to copy client content;
- product hypotheses are frozen.

## Do not delay TO merely because
- all system reports are not yet available;
- full Proclaim configuration audit is incomplete;
- economics are not final.

Those are triangulation/follow-up streams.

## PAUSE if
- observer would need covert capture;
- client-identifiable/privileged data cannot be avoided safely;
- observation is being used for named employee performance management rather than process research;
- there is no authority to observe/export the proposed data.
