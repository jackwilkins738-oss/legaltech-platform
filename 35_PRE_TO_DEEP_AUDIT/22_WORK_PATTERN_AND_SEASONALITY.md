# WORK-PATTERN / SEASONALITY CONTROL

Record whether the observation period is representative across:
- weekday;
- start/middle/end of day;
- month/quarter/end-of-month;
- staff absence/leave;
- unusually busy/quiet period;
- court/deadline spikes;
- matter-age/complexity mix;
- seasonal claim/client patterns.

Do not “correct” a narrow sample by guesswork. Attach the limitation and request corroborating data.
