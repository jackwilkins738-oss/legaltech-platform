# ECONOMIC SOURCE PACK

For each role/economic claim capture the source:
- salary or loaded cost;
- employer on-cost assumptions;
- productive working days;
- overtime/contractors;
- vacancy/hiring pressure;
- matters per handler;
- fee/funding model where relevant;
- disbursement/capital exposure where relevant;
- support/consultant spend;
- software/licence spend.

Never turn capacity into cash without a realisation path.
