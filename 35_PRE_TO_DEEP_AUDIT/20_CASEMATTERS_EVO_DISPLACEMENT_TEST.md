# CASEMATTERS EVO DISPLACEMENT TEST

Ask the firm:
- have they seen/demoed Evo?;
- contractual eligibility/timing?;
- migration effort/cost?;
- which current pains they expect Evo to solve?;
- which pains remain operational/process-specific?;
- what would make them choose an augmentation layer before/alongside Evo?

Any wedge that Evo plausibly eliminates within the buyer's decision horizon needs a stronger reason to exist.
