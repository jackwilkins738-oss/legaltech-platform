# TRAINING / ADOPTION MATURITY

Ask:
- when each role last received Proclaim training;
- whether advanced users/admin champions exist;
- which workflows are routinely bypassed;
- why users bypass them;
- whether different people perform the same task differently;
- what personal shortcuts new starters are taught;
- whether “Proclaim cannot do it” has been verified by an admin/developer.

Do not build software to compensate for a training issue unless the training burden itself is structurally unacceptable.
