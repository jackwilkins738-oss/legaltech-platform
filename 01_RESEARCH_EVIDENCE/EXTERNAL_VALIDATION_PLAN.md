# EXTERNAL VALIDATION PLAN

## Minimum initial sample
Aim for 5–10 Proclaim firms and 2+ roles per firm where possible.

## Ask
- Which workflows consume most admin time?
- What happens outside Proclaim?
- What requires workarounds?
- What changes are expensive/slow?
- What breaks?
- What is Proclaim genuinely good at?
- Which pain is severe enough to pay to remove?
- What blocks an automation layer?
- What security/procurement requirements block a pilot?
- What integrations are mandatory?
- What would make you replace Proclaim?
- What would make you refuse?

## Validation levels
Weak: 'sounds useful'
Medium: 'we have that problem'
Strong: workflow/data access offered
Very strong: controlled pilot offered
Commercial proof: willingness to pay subject to conditions

Compliments are not validation.
