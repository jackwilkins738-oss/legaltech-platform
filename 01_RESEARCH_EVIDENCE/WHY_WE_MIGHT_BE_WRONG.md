# WHY WE MIGHT BE WRONG

## Thesis
There is a large opportunity to build a materially better workflow/automation layer for Proclaim users and potentially evolve into a full legal practice-management replacement.

## Ways this could be wrong
- Problems may be firm configuration rather than Proclaim itself.
- CaseMatters Evo may resolve major pain.
- Firms may complain but refuse to switch.
- Migration risk may outweigh workflow savings.
- Procurement/security may make adoption too slow.
- Better Proclaim configuration may solve the problem cheaply.
- Users may hate clicks but buyers may not assign economic value to them.
- AI error rates may make automation too risky.
- Integration access may be insufficient.
- Support/implementation costs may destroy margins.
- Incumbents may copy obvious automation.
- The partner's branch may be unrepresentative.

## Required disproof work
Find strong Proclaim implementations, at least one satisfied customer, workflows Proclaim automates well, switching objections, competitor strengths, and real willingness-to-pay evidence.
