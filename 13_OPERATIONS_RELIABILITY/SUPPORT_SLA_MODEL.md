# SUPPORT & SLA MODEL

## Support tiers

### Standard
Business hours
normal product support

### Enterprise
extended/24x7 path for severe incidents
named escalation
customer success/technical contact

## Severity

### P1
Security incident / platform unavailable / material data integrity risk.

### P2
Major function unavailable with broad customer impact.

### P3
Limited defect/workaround available.

### P4
Question/configuration/request.

## Example response targets
Do not contract until operational capacity exists.

P1:
acknowledge within 30–60 minutes enterprise path.

P2:
within several business hours.

P3/P4:
business-day targets.

## SLA
Define:
- measured service
- exclusions
- planned maintenance
- Microsoft/Proclaim upstream dependency
- service credits
- claim process

## Important
Do not promise resolution times outside our control.

Separate:
- our uptime
- Microsoft outage
- customer's Proclaim outage
- customer network

## Customer success
Separate support from:
- onboarding
- workflow optimisation
- ROI review
- expansion

## Product differentiation
Transparent status and ownership can be a competitive advantage if Proclaim support frustration is validated.
