# VALIDATION GATES

## Problem gate
PASS:
3 unrelated firms describe materially similar workflow pain.

## Economics gate
PASS:
2 firms can quantify meaningful capacity/cycle-time/risk value.

## Access gate
PASS:
2 firms see a plausible controlled technical-access path.

## Buyer gate
PASS:
2 budget owners acknowledge the problem is buyable.

## Pilot gate
PASS:
1 firm agrees to scope a paid pilot after technical proof.

## Anti-bias gate
At least one interview must come from:
- strong internal Proclaim capability, or
- a user who is broadly satisfied with Proclaim.

## Overall
Do not proceed to production build because of:
- compliments;
- demo excitement;
- "AI sounds interesting";
- LinkedIn replies.

Proceed because of evidence + access + buyer + pilot path.
