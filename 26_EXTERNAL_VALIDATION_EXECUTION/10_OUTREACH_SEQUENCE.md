# VALIDATION OUTREACH SEQUENCE

## Touch 1 — research request
Subject/theme:
Operational research on Proclaim claims workflows.

Message:
"We're researching where manual operational work still sits around Proclaim in UK claims teams — particularly Outlook, document handling, actions and external chasing. I'm looking for 20–30 minutes with people who actually understand the workflow. This isn't a request to replace Proclaim or a generic AI demo. Would you be open to comparing how your team handles it?"

## Touch 2
Reference one relevant public fact:
- Proclaim use;
- PI/claims team;
- internal Proclaim function.

Ask one concrete question.

## Touch 3
Offer to share anonymised findings:
"We're comparing where strong Proclaim implementations still need manual work. Happy to share the aggregated findings afterward."

## Technical persona
Lead with:
"We are specifically trying to distinguish product limitations from configuration/resource limitations."

## Rule
No fake urgency.
No invented customer proof.
No claims of quantified savings before TO/pilot evidence.
