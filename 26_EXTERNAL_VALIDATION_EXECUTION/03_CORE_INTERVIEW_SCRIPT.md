# CORE 30-MINUTE INTERVIEW

## Opening — 2 min
"We're studying operational work around Proclaim in UK claims teams. We're not trying to sell you a replacement system on this call. We're trying to understand where the real manual work still sits."

## Current environment — 4 min
- Which teams use Proclaim?
- Roughly how many users?
- What practice areas?
- How customised is it?
- Internal Proclaim resource or external consultant?

## Workflow — 10 min
"Take one common inbound email/document. What happens from arrival until the matter is updated and the next action exists?"

Probe:
- Outlook?
- find matter?
- classify?
- attachments?
- re-key?
- create task?
- inform client?
- supervisor?
- exception?

Then:
"What workflow wastes the most admin time each week?"

## Economics — 4 min
- Roughly how many people do it?
- How often?
- What happens when it is delayed?
- Does it create overtime, hiring pressure, complaints or slower settlements?

## Current automation — 4 min
- What have you already automated?
- What does Proclaim do particularly well?
- What looks manual but could be fixed by configuration?
- What have you wanted changed but not implemented?

## Access / feasibility — 3 min
"If a supplier worked alongside Proclaim rather than replacing it, would your IT team consider controlled Microsoft Graph and Proclaim API/service access for a limited pilot?"

Probe why yes/no.

## Buying — 2 min
"If a pilot demonstrably removed this work without changing Proclaim, who would need to approve it?"

## Disconfirmation — 1 min
"If you had to argue that this problem isn't worth solving externally, what would your strongest argument be?"

## Rule
Do not pitch through uncomfortable answers.
Record them.
