# BUYER / ROI INTERVIEW

## Goal
Determine whether pain becomes a budget.

## Ask
- What operational KPI matters most?
- Current staffing pressure?
- Planned hires?
- Average caseload/FTE?
- Where are supervisors spending time?
- What creates complaints?
- What causes avoidable delays?
- How do you approve software spend?
- Who owns budget?
- Typical pilot/procurement path?
- What would make a £5k–£15k pilot obviously worthwhile?
- What annual result could justify £25k/£50k/£100k?

## Price testing
Do not ask:
"Would you pay £50k?"

Ask:
"If this reliably released the equivalent of 1.5 FTE of capacity across this team, how would you normally value/approve that?"

Then test budget ranges.

## Evidence
Separate:
- polite interest;
- stated problem;
- budget owner;
- quantified value;
- actual next step.
