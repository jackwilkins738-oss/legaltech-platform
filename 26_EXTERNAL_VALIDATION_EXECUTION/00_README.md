# EXTERNAL VALIDATION EXECUTION

## Goal
Test whether the partner firm's workflow pain generalises across unrelated UK firms using Proclaim or adjacent legacy legal systems.

## Minimum evidence gate
Do not call the problem externally validated until:
- 5+ useful interviews;
- 3+ unrelated firms independently report materially similar workflow pain;
- at least 2 plausible budget owners acknowledge economic value;
- at least 2 firms consider controlled technical access plausible;
- at least 1 firm is willing to discuss a paid pilot after technical proof.

Target 10 interviews for stronger confidence.

## Interview mix
Include:
- high-volume PI;
- strong internal Proclaim team;
- satisfied/defensive Proclaim user;
- firm considering Evo/replacement;
- operations leader;
- technical/application owner.

## Anti-bias
Every interview must ask:
"If you had to defend Proclaim on this workflow, what does it do well?"
