# OBJECTION / DISCONFIRMATION MAP

## "Proclaim already does this"
Action:
ask to see/describe implementation, cost, resource and adoption.
Outcome may be:
- kill feature;
- partner with Proclaim consultants;
- position around configuration/change speed.

## "We're moving to Evo"
Test:
timeline, confidence, migration scope, whether workflow issue survives.

## "Microsoft Copilot will solve it"
Test:
does Copilot perform governed matter matching/writeback/audit/exceptions?

## "We won't give mailbox access"
Explore:
- mailbox-specific scope;
- customer-hosted component;
- Outlook add-in;
- manual upload.
If nobody accepts access, inbound wedge may fail.

## "Human review makes it pointless"
Measure review seconds/minutes and exception rate.
Do not argue theoretically.

## "We can build it internally"
Ask:
- backlog;
- opportunity cost;
- maintenance;
- specialist resource.
Strong internal teams are valuable disconfirmation targets.

## "Startup risk"
Mitigation:
- narrow pilot;
- exportability;
- no replacement;
- customer-owned system remains source of record;
- strong security controls.

## "No budget"
Determine whether:
- pain is weak;
- wrong buyer;
- timing;
- value not quantified.
