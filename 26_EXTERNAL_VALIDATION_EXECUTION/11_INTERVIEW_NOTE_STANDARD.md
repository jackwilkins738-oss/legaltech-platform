# INTERVIEW NOTE STANDARD

## Header
- firm
- date
- role
- interviewer
- evidence source for Proclaim/practice
- permission for follow-up

## Record claims as
- DIRECT USER REPORT
- VERIFIED FACT
- HYPOTHESIS

## Workflow
Capture:
trigger → steps → systems → frequency → people → exceptions → consequence.

## Exact useful phrases
Short quotations may be captured with permission for internal research, but do not treat anecdotes as quantitative truth.

## End
- problem score
- repeatability score
- economics score
- access score
- buyer score
- pilot score
- total
- strongest confirming evidence
- strongest disconfirming evidence
- next action

## Privacy
Do not record client-identifying case details.
