# V1 — INBOUND LEGAL OPERATIONS REQUIREMENTS

## Product objective
Reduce manual administration generated by inbound legal email and documents while preserving confidentiality and human legal judgement.

## Inputs
V1 supports:
- Outlook/Exchange email
- email attachments
- manually uploaded documents

Later:
- portals
- scans
- APIs
- client portal
- third-party feeds

## Core workflow

### 1. Receive
Graph notification / upload.

### 2. Secure ingest
- tenant validation
- malware scan
- MIME/type validation
- size limits
- quarantine
- dedupe

### 3. Parse
Extract:
- sender
- recipients
- subject
- body
- attachments
- dates
- references

### 4. Matter match
Return:
- candidate matter(s)
- evidence/signals
- confidence

### 5. Classify
Examples:
- client correspondence
- defendant correspondence
- insurer response
- medical record
- medical report
- expert correspondence
- court document
- offer
- invoice/disbursement
- identity
- signed authority

### 6. Extract structured information
Examples:
- names
- claim reference
- date
- amount
- appointment
- admission/denial
- deadline candidate
- missing-document response

### 7. Detect work
System proposes:
- task
- reminder
- follow-up
- deadline review
- field update
- draft response

### 8. Risk policy
Classify proposed action:
- read-only
- metadata
- reversible internal action
- external action
- high-impact/legal

### 9. Human review
Queue only items requiring:
- uncertain match
- uncertain classification
- legal decision
- outbound approval
- deadline confirmation
- conflict/restricted matter handling

### 10. Write-back
Approved actions:
- canonical system
- Proclaim where appropriate

### 11. Audit
Record:
- source
- decision
- confidence
- user approval
- output
- write-back result

## V1 user interface
### Inbox / exception queue
Each card:
- matter
- sender
- classification
- proposed action
- confidence
- reason
- risk
- approve/edit/reject

### Matter view
- latest communications
- documents
- tasks
- deadlines
- exceptions

## Explicit V1 exclusions
- autonomous legal advice
- autonomous court filing
- client-money movement
- full accounts
- full CRM
- full Proclaim replacement
- unrestricted autonomous sending
