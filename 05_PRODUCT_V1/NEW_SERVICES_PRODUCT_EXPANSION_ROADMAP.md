# NEW SERVICES PRODUCT EXPANSION ROADMAP

## Core thesis
Do not build another generic PMS. Build the **legal operations execution layer** that works alongside Proclaim first, removes measurable administration, accumulates canonical matter context and later becomes capable of replacing the legacy core.

## Priority 1 — highest-value new services

### 1. Matter Command / Case Health & Risk Radar
Continuously detect:
- overdue tasks,
- missing evidence,
- stale correspondence,
- unanswered third parties,
- candidate deadlines,
- failed workflows,
- inconsistent data,
- overdue client updates.

Output: **what needs attention today, and why?**

Why: CARET, Clio, Litify and Actionstep all move toward proactive attention management.

**Recommendation: BUILD NEXT.**

### 2. Evidence & Records Orchestration
For PI/clinical negligence:
- generate record requests,
- track providers,
- chase,
- ingest records,
- validate date ranges,
- detect missing sets,
- classify/index,
- escalate.

Why: Filevine's medical-record strategy shows retrieval is a valuable workflow service.

**Recommendation: BUILD after TO validates volume; partner for fulfilment initially.**

### 3. Firm Skills / Workflow Agent Builder
Let authorised ops teams define firm-standard behaviours:
- how an insurer denial is handled,
- required evidence,
- client-update conventions,
- escalation,
- approvals.

Compile into constrained, versioned workflow actions rather than free-form autonomous code.

Why: Clio Vincent Skills + Harvey custom agents.

**Recommendation: BUILD as a core moat.**

### 4. Matter Intelligence Workspace
Permission-aware Q&A over matter contents:
- what happened?
- what are we waiting for?
- what changed?
- what did the insurer admit?
- build chronology.
Every answer cites sources.

Why: now table stakes across leading platforms.

**Recommendation: BUILD, but action-oriented rather than chatbot-first.**

### 5. Evidence Completeness Engine
Understand what should exist for the matter type/stage and compare it with what actually exists.

Example:
"GP records received; hospital records missing for the treatment period."

Why: combines workflow + documents + domain model; harder to commoditise than summarisation.

**Recommendation: BUILD.**

## Priority 2 — PI-specific operating depth

### 6. Medical Chronology Builder
Structured treatment chronology with page/source citations and gap detection.

**Guardrail:** factual organisation, not autonomous causation opinion.

### 7. Liability / Offer Intelligence
Extract:
- admission/denial,
- split liability,
- offer/counteroffer,
- Part 36 indicators where relevant,
- dates and response periods.

**Guardrail:** structure facts; do not independently advise settlement acceptance.

### 8. Demand / Settlement Pack Builder
Assemble factual case summary, liability position, medical evidence, losses, chronology and supporting documents into a reviewable pack.

Adapt to UK PI procedure rather than copying US demand-letter workflows.

### 9. Court & Deadline Intelligence
- court order extraction,
- source-linked candidate deadlines,
- human confirmation,
- later deterministic rules,
- court/docket integration where UK services permit.

## Priority 3 — client and growth services

### 10. Smart Client Updates + Secure Portal
Detect meaningful events and prepare plain-English updates.
Portal for:
- status,
- uploads,
- messages,
- appointments,
- approved documents.

### 11. Intake & Lead Conversion
- web/call intake,
- qualification,
- conflict/pre-check,
- source attribution,
- follow-up,
- evidence request,
- matter creation with zero re-keying.

### 12. Voice Intake / Call Intelligence
AI call assistant or voice agent using firm-approved scripts.

**Recommendation:** partner for voice infrastructure; own legal workflow/policy.

## Priority 4 — firm-wide operations

### 13. Cross-Matter Operations Intelligence
Answer:
- where are cases stuck?
- which provider causes delays?
- which stage has longest dwell?
- which team is overloaded?
- where is automation failing?

### 14. Capacity & Workload Engine
Use task volume, due dates, case complexity and historical throughput to route and forecast workload.

### 15. Passive Work / Revenue Capture
Detect activity and suggest time/cost/work entries.
Useful even for non-hourly PI because it reveals true matter cost and team capacity.

## Priority 5 — platform and ecosystem

### 16. Practice Pack Marketplace
Reusable PI/clinical-negligence/etc packages:
- fields,
- stages,
- document classes,
- task types,
- workflows,
- dashboards,
- extraction schemas,
- KPI definitions.

### 17. Integration Marketplace / API
Connect specialist providers:
- e-sign,
- AML/KYC,
- records,
- telephony,
- payments,
- court services,
- experts,
- BI.

### 18. Migration-as-a-Service Product
Discovery, mapping, hashes, workflow inventory, dry run, delta migration, reconciliation and rollback.

This is a strategic moat against Proclaim.

### 19. Microsoft-Native Workspace
Outlook:
- file + create task + propose reply.
Word:
- pull verified matter facts + save new version.
Teams:
- exception alerts/actions.

### 20. Legal Operations Engineering
High-value implementation service:
- map workflow,
- configure agents,
- create evaluations,
- integrate,
- measure ROI.

Constraint: no permanent bespoke code per customer.

## Build vs partner

### Build ourselves
- case health/exception engine,
- workflow agents,
- matter intelligence,
- evidence completeness,
- PI domain model,
- cross-matter intelligence,
- migration engine,
- canonical data layer.

### Partner/integrate first
- legal research databases,
- medical-record fulfilment network,
- AML/KYC,
- e-sign,
- payments,
- telephony/voice infrastructure,
- court filing/data where possible.

## Do not prioritise early
- full legal accounting,
- generic contract AI,
- general legal research database,
- autonomous legal advice,
- generic "ChatGPT for your firm."

## Recommended sequence

### Phase 1
Inbound Operations → Exception Queue → Matter Command → Firm Skills → Evidence Completeness.

### Phase 2
Records Orchestration → Matter Intelligence → Client Updates → Medical Chronology → Liability/Offer Intelligence → Deadline Intelligence.

### Phase 3
Settlement Pack → Intake → Cross-Matter Intelligence → Capacity Engine.

### Phase 4
Microsoft-native surfaces → Practice Pack Marketplace → Integration Marketplace → Migration-as-a-Service → Legal Operations Engineering.

### Phase 5
Only after evidence: broader PMS displacement.

## Product position
Not: **AI legal case management.**

Better:
**The legal operations platform that runs the work around every matter.**

For Proclaim firms initially:
**Keep Proclaim. Remove the admin around it.**

Long term:
**Every matter tells the platform what needs to happen next. Routine work runs automatically; people handle judgment and exceptions.**
