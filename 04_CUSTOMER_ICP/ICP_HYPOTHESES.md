# ICP HYPOTHESES

## Primary hypothesis
UK law firms:
- 50–250 Proclaim users
- Microsoft 365
- high-volume process-driven practice areas
- central admin/case-management teams
- operations/IT leadership
- repeated inbound email/document workload
- meaningful Proclaim customisation
- frustration with change/support/performance

## Candidate practice areas
Score first:
1. Personal injury
2. Clinical negligence
3. Conveyancing
4. Housing/disrepair
5. Employment
6. Volume litigation/debt
7. Immigration
8. Family

## What makes a practice area attractive
- repeated workflow structure
- high document/email volume
- clear stage progression
- frequent chasing
- predictable missing-information states
- large number of matters
- clear deadlines
- admin burden that can be separated from legal judgement

## What makes a practice area unattractive initially
- highly bespoke low-volume work
- every matter progresses differently
- major legal judgement at each step
- low administrative volume
- very small teams
- complex client-money requirements required in V1
