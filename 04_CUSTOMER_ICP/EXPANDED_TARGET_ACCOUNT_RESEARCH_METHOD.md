# EXPANDED TARGET-ACCOUNT RESEARCH METHOD

## Goal
Expand from the verified seed list to 20–30 evidence-backed accounts, then eventually 100.

## Research channels
1. current firm careers pages
2. current job adverts
3. Access/Proclaim case studies
4. Proclaim integration partner references
5. recruitment adverts requiring Proclaim
6. staff role profiles where publicly available
7. company growth/acquisition news
8. current practice-area pages

## Account categories

### Tier A
Current direct evidence of Proclaim + high-volume PI/clinical negligence.

### Tier B
Current direct Proclaim evidence + adjacent litigation.

### Tier C
Strong current indirect Proclaim evidence.

### Tier D
High-volume PI firm, Proclaim unknown.

## Prioritise
- 50–250 likely users
- PI/RTA/EL/PL/clinical negligence
- large case-handler teams
- paperless/digital claims
- active hiring
- operational growth
- multiple branches
- obvious IT/ops leadership

## Fields
- firm
- domain
- HQ
- branch count
- practice area
- current Proclaim proof
- proof date
- proof URL
- headcount estimate
- team-size evidence
- caseload evidence
- buyer candidates
- commercial trigger
- score
- outreach readiness

## Research rule
A recruiter advert alone is not enough for "verified Proclaim customer."
Reverify against firm/current employee/company evidence when possible.
