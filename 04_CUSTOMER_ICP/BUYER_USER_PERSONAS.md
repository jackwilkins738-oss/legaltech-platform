# BUYER & USER PERSONAS

## Economic buyer — COO / Operations Director

### Cares about
- cost per matter
- staff utilisation
- capacity
- throughput
- service quality
- operational risk
- scalability
- headcount pressure

### Pain
- adding staff because processes do not scale
- weak management visibility
- work queues
- rework
- delays
- software change dependency

### Message
"Increase matter capacity without increasing administrative headcount at the same rate."

## IT Director / Head of IT

### Cares about
- architecture
- integrations
- security
- support
- migration
- maintainability
- vendor stability
- identity

### Pain
- desktop dependencies
- Office compatibility
- custom scripts
- support tickets
- vendor changes
- fragile integrations

### Message
"Replace fragile desktop workflow with governed API-first automation."

## Managing Partner / CEO

### Cares about
- profitability
- growth
- reputation
- risk
- strategic platform choice

### Message
"Increase capacity and improve client service while reducing operational dependency on legacy software."

## Practice Head

### Cares about
- fee-earner performance
- deadlines
- case progression
- supervision
- client outcomes

### Message
"Surface stalled/risky matters automatically instead of relying on manual supervision."

## Team Leader / Supervisor

### Cares about
- overdue work
- workload
- quality
- exceptions
- staff support

### Product requirement
Exception dashboard, not just reports.

## Case Manager / Fee Earner

### Cares about
- progressing cases
- legal work
- avoiding admin
- finding information quickly
- not missing deadlines

### Product requirement
One work queue with context and next actions.

## Legal Admin

### Cares about
- speed
- repetitive work
- clear instructions
- low error rate

### Product requirement
Automation of:
- filing
- data entry
- document classification
- standard correspondence
- chasing

## Security / DPO / Compliance
Potential blocker.

### Cares about
- client confidentiality
- GDPR
- subprocessors
- audit
- AI
- access
- data residency
- incident response

### Message
"Automation is permission-bounded, auditable and designed for legal confidentiality."
