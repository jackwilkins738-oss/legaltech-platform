# AI data boundary before and during TO

STATUS: CURRENT
AUTHORITY: HIGH

Until authorised customer/firm data-processing arrangements are confirmed:
- no client names;
- no addresses/DOBs/contact details;
- no diagnoses/medical content;
- no privileged/legal advice text;
- no full email bodies;
- no unredacted documents;
- no passwords, tokens, API keys or production secrets;
- no screenshots containing client data;
- no real mailbox or production Proclaim credentials in Claude knowledge/repo.

Use pseudonymous matter/event identifiers and workflow descriptions.

If representative content is later authorised, use the customer-approved environment and data-handling process rather than weakening this rule informally.
