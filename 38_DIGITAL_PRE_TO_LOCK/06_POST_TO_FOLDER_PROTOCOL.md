# Post-TO evidence folder protocol

STATUS: CURRENT
AUTHORITY: HIGH

Use `39_POST_TO_EVIDENCE/` only.

- `00_RAW_IMMUTABLE/`: untouched exports/notes/session copies.
- `01_CLEANED_NORMALISED/`: anonymised/normalised data plus transformation notes.
- `02_ANALYSIS/`: timing distributions, task-family analysis, volume/economics, root causes.
- `03_DECISIONS/`: evidence-gated decision records.
- `04_PRODUCT_CHANGES/`: PRD/roadmap/backlog consequences after decisions.
- `05_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION/`: interview/system evidence used to triangulate TO findings.
- `06_INTEGRATION_EVIDENCE/`: authorised Proclaim/Graph/technical findings.

Never place raw evidence directly into product folders.
