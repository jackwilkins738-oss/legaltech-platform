# Strategy Change Gate

STATUS: CURRENT
AUTHORITY: HIGH

No raw observation may directly change strategy.

Required chain:

RAW EVIDENCE
→ QUALITY / PRIVACY CHECK
→ CLEANED / NORMALISED DATA
→ COVERAGE / LIMITATIONS REVIEW
→ TASK-FAMILY ANALYSIS
→ VOLUME TRIANGULATION
→ ROOT-CAUSE DIAGNOSIS
→ PROCLAIM / MICROSOFT / CHEAPEST-FIX TEST
→ ECONOMICS + RISK
→ HYPOTHESIS FALSIFICATION
→ DECISION LOG
→ PRODUCT / ROADMAP CHANGE

## Blocked behaviour
Claude must not, from raw notes alone:
- declare a wedge validated;
- create a production feature priority;
- update pricing;
- change automation risk thresholds;
- claim annual savings;
- tell engineering to build a customer-facing feature;
- remove a competing hypothesis;
- generalise from one worker/session to the firm or market.

## Allowed from raw evidence
- organise and anonymise;
- flag missing fields;
- identify follow-up questions;
- calculate direct measured durations with clear caveats;
- detect contradictions;
- propose what evidence is still needed.
