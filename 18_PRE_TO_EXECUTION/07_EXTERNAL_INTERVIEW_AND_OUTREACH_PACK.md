# EXTERNAL INTERVIEW & OUTREACH PACK

## Objective
Get evidence, not compliments.

## Initial contact
Target:
- Head/Director of Operations
- Practice Head
- IT/Applications lead
- Proclaim developer/manager

## Research outreach message
Short form:

We're researching how high-volume legal teams actually work around Proclaim — particularly the manual work between Outlook, documents, tasks and case progression.

We're not looking to sell you software on the call. We're trying to understand which workflows genuinely consume time, which Proclaim already handles well, and where firms still rely on manual work.

Could I ask you 20 minutes of operational questions?

## Interview order
1. environment
2. workflow
3. economics
4. change/support
5. automation boundaries
6. buying/procurement

## Evidence questions
- What does your team do manually every day that Proclaim technically stores but does not execute for them?
- Which inboxes create the most admin?
- What do staff copy between Outlook and Proclaim?
- What do supervisors repeatedly inspect?
- Which third parties are constantly chased?
- Where do matters sit still?
- What changes require a Proclaim specialist?
- Which parts are already automated successfully?
- What did Evo change or promise?
- What would you never let software automate?

## Quantification prompts
Instead of:
"Is this annoying?"

Ask:
"How many people do this?"
"How often?"
"Roughly how long each time?"
"What happens when it isn't done?"
"Do you hire specifically to handle this?"

## Buying questions
- Who owns this cost?
- Who could approve a pilot?
- What security review is mandatory?
- Would controlled mailbox/API access be possible?
- What makes a startup unacceptable?
- What measurable result would justify paying?

## Disconfirmation question
"If you could defend Proclaim on this workflow, what does it actually do very well?"

Always ask this.
