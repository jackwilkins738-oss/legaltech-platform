- Core / Signature / Elite

### Security claims
Current Clio UK pages state:
- encryption in transit and at rest
- SOC 2 Type II
- GDPR handling
- independent third-party audits
- 99.9% uptime guarantee
- encrypted backups
- MFA

- no use of customer data by external model providers for training
