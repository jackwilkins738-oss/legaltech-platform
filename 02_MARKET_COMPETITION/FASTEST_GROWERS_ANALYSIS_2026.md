# FASTEST-GROWING LEGAL TECH — WHY THEY ARE GROWING

## Method
Private companies disclose inconsistent metrics, so this is a directional ranking using the strongest public signals: ARR, customer growth, funding/valuation, SaaS-user growth and migration volume.

## Tier 1 — extraordinary growth

### Legora
- 250 firms in May 2025.
- 400+ by October 2025.
- Financial Times reported ~1,500 customers and ~$150m ARR by Q2 2026.
- Valuation jumped from $675m in May 2025 to ~$5.55bn by March/April 2026, with later fundraising discussions above $10bn.

**Why:** collaborative enterprise workflows, rapid deployment, review/drafting/research in one environment, deep firm partnerships.

**Lesson:** workflow adoption across teams is much more valuable than individual chatbot usage.

### Harvey
- $11bn valuation March 2026.
- $15.5bn valuation September 2026.
- Company said customers were running 25,000+ custom agents by March.

**Why:** agents tailored to firm workflows, elite-firm penetration, embedded legal engineering, enterprise security.

**Lesson:** software + workflow engineering is a strong enterprise adoption model.

### Filevine
- Announced $400m financing in September 2025.
- Strong expansion into AI and adjacent litigation services.
- Third-party estimates indicate rapid ARR and account expansion; treat those estimates as non-audited.

**Why:** vertical litigation data, PI depth, ecosystem, medical-record and case-value workflows.

**Lesson:** own the vertical operations first, then monetise intelligence on top.

## Tier 2 — large platforms accelerating

### Clio
- $500m+ ARR by May 2026.
- $5bn valuation.
- $1bn vLex acquisition.
- Expansion into enterprise, courts and legal research.

**Why:** broad distribution, ecosystem, trusted platform, legal knowledge + matter context + execution.

### Actionstep
- 50+ UK firms adopted since Jan 2024 by Apr 2025.
- 30 midsize firms migrated from legacy systems.
- UK team expanded from 5 to nearly 20.

**Why:** cloud-first, flexible workflows, midmarket focus, migration capability.

### Elite
- Reported 125% YoY growth in monthly SaaS users in Nov 2025.
- 100+ major-firm cloud go-lives.

**Why:** enterprise trust and obvious financial/work-to-cash value.

## Tier 3 — strong strategic momentum

### Litify
450+ enterprise customers and 70k+ professionals claimed, with rapid 2026 agentic expansion.

### Assembly Neos
1,450+ PI firms claimed and strong adoption/ROI signals in 2026 G2 reporting.

## Shared growth drivers

1. **Vertical specificity** — software speaks the workflow's language.
2. **Context** — AI sees matter data, documents, correspondence, financial state and sometimes legal authority.
3. **Action** — systems create, route, schedule, draft, file and execute.
4. **Distribution in existing workflow** — Word, Outlook, Teams, PMS.
5. **Expansion revenue** — AI, intake, payments, analytics, research, records and court data.
6. **Migration/onboarding** — switching becomes survivable.
7. **Quantified outcomes** — faster resolution, more cases, less admin, better collections.
