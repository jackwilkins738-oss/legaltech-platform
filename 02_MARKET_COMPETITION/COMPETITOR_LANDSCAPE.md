# COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE

## Strategic point
The market already contains credible cloud-native and automation-first competitors.
The company must not assume it is competing only with Proclaim.

## Clio
Current positioning includes:
- cloud practice management
- matter management
- document management
- billing/payments
- client portal
- task/calendar
- 100+ integrations
- migration services
- AI that converts court documents to calendar events
- AI client-update drafting
- AI file naming/organisation
- AI billing workflows
- human review/approval checkpoints
- permission-aware AI

### Threat
Clio is moving from "practice management" toward an operating platform plus legal AI.
A first product that only does summaries, deadline extraction, drafting, file naming or task suggestions is not sufficiently differentiated.

## Actionstep
Current positioning includes:
- matter management
- legal accounting
- document management
- intake/CRM
- highly configurable workflows
- automated task assignment
- reminders
- matter progression
- configurable workflows by practice
- cloud delivery
- security / SSO / MFA positioning

### Threat
"Visual workflow automation for law firms" is already an established proposition.
Differentiation must come from workflow quality, ease of configuration, specific Proclaim migration/augmentation, AI exception handling, enterprise controls, or measurable labour removal.

## SOS Legal
Positions to enterprise firms with:
- practice management
- accounts
- case management
- document management
- customisation
- enterprise orientation

### Threat
Enterprise firms value customisation and accounting depth, not only UI.

## LEAP
Established UK legal PMS with strong practice/content positioning, especially smaller firms.

## Access ecosystem
Access can bundle legal products and already owns the Proclaim customer relationship.

## Competitive positioning rule
Do not position as:
- cloud legal software
- AI legal software
- workflow automation
- matter management
These are categories, not differentiation.

## Potential differentiation directions
1. Best Proclaim-to-modern migration engine
2. Best inbound information automation
3. Exception-based legal operations
4. Operational intelligence for high-volume legal teams
5. Extreme admin labour removal with auditable automation
6. Enterprise-grade configurability without specialist developers
7. Security/confidentiality architecture designed for complex matter-level access
8. Fast deployment alongside Proclaim before replacement
