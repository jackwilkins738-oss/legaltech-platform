# OPEN QUESTIONS

## TO / customer evidence — BLOCKING PRODUCT DECISION
- What workflow consumes the most total annual staff capacity?
- What is the real exception/ambiguity rate?
- What is the actual error/rework rate?
- Which wedge creates enough ROI to support enterprise ACV?
- Who will pay, and how much?
- Will users trust/adopt the review workflow?

## Partner Proclaim environment — BLOCKING TECHNICAL CONFIDENCE
- Which API/module entitlements exist at the partner firm?
- Which read/write operations are officially supported in that environment?
- What throughput/limits/version differences exist?
- Does the firm intend to move to CaseMatters Evo?
- What custom fields/workflows/integrations must be preserved?

## External customer validation
- Are the partner firm's pains repeated across 5–10 unrelated firms?
- What prevents firms granting Graph/Proclaim access?
- What procurement/security requirements appear first?
- What would make firms refuse a pilot?

## AI/OCR — REQUIRES REAL EVALUATION DATA
- Which provider/model wins against representative legal documents?
- Which OCR provider performs best on real scans/records at acceptable cost/residency?

## Commercial — REQUIRES BUYER EVIDENCE
- Exact pilot fee?
- Sustainable annual pricing?
- Customer-required insurance limits?
- Real sales-cycle/procurement duration?
