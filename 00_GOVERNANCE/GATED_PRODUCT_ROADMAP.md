# GATED PRODUCT ROADMAP

## STAGE 0 — Research
Build:
nothing broad.

Must prove:
- market/problem
- Proclaim fit
- ICP
- workflow opportunity

Gate:
external confirmation + TO later.

---

## STAGE 1 — Technical spike
Build:
- Graph ingest
- safe attachment pipeline
- simple canonical matter model
- Proclaim read/write proof
- audit

Gate:
end-to-end technically works.

---

## STAGE 2 — Matching/classification prototype
Build:
- matter matcher
- classifier
- review queue
- evaluation harness

Gate:
accuracy/safety thresholds.

---

## STAGE 3 — Paid pilot product
Build:
- production auth
- tenancy
- approvals
- write-back
- monitoring
- basic reporting
- security baseline

Gate:
paid customer + measured value.

---

## STAGE 4 — Repeatable wedge
Build:
- configuration
- onboarding tooling
- workflow engine
- customer admin
- stronger reporting
- support

Gate:
multiple firms with repeatable implementation.

---

## STAGE 5 — Operations platform
Build:
- exception management
- task/deadline intelligence
- document intelligence
- client intake
- management analytics

Gate:
customers operate materially through us.

---

## STAGE 6 — Migration capability
Build:
- scanner
- mapping
- reconciliation
- dry run
- parallel run

Gate:
customer willing to migrate core workflows/data.

---

## STAGE 7 — PMS replacement
Build only proven missing capabilities.

Exclude accounting unless independently justified.

Gate:
first full production replacement.

---

## STAGE 8 — Scale
- certifications
- partner ecosystem
- vertical templates
- sales team
- SRE/security
- international only when justified

## Rule
Each stage can be killed or redirected.
No stage exists because it appeared in an original founder plan.
