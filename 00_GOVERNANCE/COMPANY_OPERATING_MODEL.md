# COMPANY OPERATING MODEL — 2 FOUNDERS TO SCALE

## Stage 0 — two founders

### Founder A — Product / Commercial
Owns:
- customer research
- product requirements
- market
- sales
- pricing
- customer relationships

### Founder B — Technical
Owns:
- engineering
- architecture
- infrastructure
- integrations
- technical operations

## Shared / named responsibility
Even with two founders assign an owner for:
- security
- privacy
- finance
- incident response
- vendor management
- documentation

## Weekly operating cadence

### Monday
- customer evidence
- product priorities
- risks
- sprint commitment

### Daily
short engineering/product sync.

### Friday
- shipped
- customer learning
- incidents
- metrics
- decisions
- documentation update

## Decision logs
Material decisions get ADR/decision record:
- problem
- options
- evidence
- choice
- downside
- revisit trigger

## First likely hires
Depends on traction, but likely:
1. strong full-stack/backend engineer
2. legal operations/domain specialist
3. implementation/customer success
4. security/compliance capability
5. sales once founder-led sales repeatable

## What not to hire early
Large sales team before repeatable close.
Large support team before product usage.
Dedicated compliance bureaucracy before controls/product need.

## Scale stages

### 0–10 customers
founder-led sales
founder product
technical founder architecture
high-touch onboarding

### 10–50
implementation function
customer success
security formalisation
SRE/on-call

### 50–200
sales team
product teams
security leader
support tiers
formal compliance

### 200+
regional/vertical teams
platform engineering
SOC/security operations
partner ecosystem
