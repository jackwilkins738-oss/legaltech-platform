# PROJECT DECISION RIGHTS

## Founder strategic decisions
Market, practice area, pricing, funding, hiring, jurisdiction expansion, full Proclaim replacement timing, high-risk legal accounting scope.

## Product-owner decisions
UX priorities, workflow requirements, V1 inclusion, research priorities, product metrics.

## Engineering-owner decisions
Implementation details and tooling inside approved architecture.

## Security-sensitive decisions requiring explicit review
Authentication, authorisation, tenant isolation, encryption/keys, sensitive logging, production access, exports, AI tool permissions, subprocessors, international processing, backup/restore.

## Claude rule
Claude may recommend but is not the decision owner. Material architecture and security decisions must be recorded.
