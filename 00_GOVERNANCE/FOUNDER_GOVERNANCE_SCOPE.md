# FOUNDER GOVERNANCE SCOPE

## Decisions to document
- Legal entity and share ownership
- Vesting and leaver treatment
- IP assignment to the company
- Founder roles: CEO/business, product, engineering, security, compliance/privacy, finance, sales, customer research, infrastructure, incident response
- Decisions either founder may make alone
- Decisions requiring both founders
- Technical architecture owner
- Product-scope owner
- Security veto authority
- Spend, hiring and contract-signing authority
- Deadlock mechanism
- Founder departure process
- Credential/access revocation

## Company-controlled systems
The company should ultimately control GitHub, domains/DNS, Vercel/cloud, Supabase/database, email/Microsoft tenant, AI/API accounts, password manager, secrets manager, billing, monitoring and support systems.

## Gate
No production customer data until control of critical company systems and IP is unambiguous.
