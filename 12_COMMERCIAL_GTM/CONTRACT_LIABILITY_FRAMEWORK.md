# CONTRACT / LIABILITY FRAMEWORK

## Not legal advice
Final agreements require qualified UK technology/privacy counsel.

## Contract set
- MSA
- Order Form
- DPA
- Security Schedule/TOMs
- SLA
- Subprocessor terms/list
- Acceptable Use
- Support policy
- AI-specific terms where needed

## Key commercial provisions
- term
- renewal
- fees
- implementation
- usage
- suspension
- termination
- transition assistance

## Liability structure
Must align with insurance and risk.

Common SaaS negotiation areas:
- overall liability cap
- data protection/security cap or super-cap
- IP indemnity
- confidentiality
- excluded/uncapped liabilities where law requires
- indirect/consequential loss exclusions

Do not casually accept unlimited liability for:
- all data incidents
- all AI errors
- all missed legal deadlines
without specialist advice and insurance alignment.

## AI responsibility allocation
Customer remains responsible for professional legal judgement.
System:
- assists
- proposes
- automates approved administrative actions

High-impact legal decisions require human authority unless explicitly separately agreed and controlled.

## Accuracy
No promise of perfect AI classification/extraction.

Contract should tie service to:
- documented functionality
- acceptance/pilot criteria
- reasonable care/security commitments
not outcome guarantees.

## Data
Cover:
- ownership
- licence to process
- return/export
- deletion
- retention
- subprocessors
- international transfers
