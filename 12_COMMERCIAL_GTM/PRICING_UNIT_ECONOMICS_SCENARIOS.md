# PRICING & UNIT ECONOMICS SCENARIOS

## Competitive anchors

### Proclaim public benchmark
51–100 user core licence:
~£73/user/month, excluding hosting/VAT in published G-Cloud pricing.

Separate hosting may materially increase TCO.

### Clio
Current UK Core:
starts around £79/user/month.
Higher tiers / AI packages are quote-based.

### Actionstep
Pricing:
per user + implementation fees; quote-based.

## Do not compete solely on cheaper seats
That creates:
- price comparison
- weak moat
- pressure on margins
- wrong customer expectation

## Initial wedge pricing scenarios

### Scenario A — Pilot
£5k–£15k fixed paid pilot
for one team/workflow.

Goal:
prove ROI, not maximise pilot revenue.

### Scenario B — Department platform
£20k–£40k ARR
depending on:
- users
- automation volume
- workflow count

### Scenario C — Firm-wide operations layer
£40k–£100k+ ARR
for 100+ user firms if ROI supports it.

These are hypotheses.

## Unit economics targets
Long term:
- software gross margin ideally >75%
- implementation should not become permanent low-margin consulting
- onboarding payback included in ACV
- AI cost visible per tenant/workflow

Track:
- AI cost/email
- AI cost/document

- expensive model usage
- caching savings

## Example ROI logic
If a 100-user firm saves:
15 min/user/day
≈ 25 staff hours/day
≈ 5,500 hours/year at 220 days.

At £25 loaded hourly cost:
~£137,500 annual capacity value.

A £40k annual product could still create >3x gross capacity value.

This is illustrative only.
TO data must replace assumptions.
