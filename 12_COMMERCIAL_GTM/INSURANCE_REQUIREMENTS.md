# INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

## Obtain broker/legal advice before enterprise launch.

## Likely policies

### Technology Errors & Omissions / Professional Indemnity
Risk:
- software failure
- implementation error
- integration error
- professional technology service claim

### Cyber Liability
Risk:
- privacy breach
- incident response
- ransomware
- regulatory/forensic costs
- notification
- cyber business interruption

### Public / Employers Liability
As company hires and operates normally.

### Directors & Officers
More relevant as investment/board complexity grows.

## Coverage review must include
- law-firm clients
- confidential legal data
- special-category health data
- AI-enabled product
- outsourced cloud
- subcontractors
- incident response panel
- retroactive date
- territorial coverage
- contractual liability
- business interruption

## Contract alignment
Never agree to liability exposure wildly beyond available insurance without deliberate founder/legal approval.

## Procurement
Expect larger firms to request certificates and minimum policy limits.
