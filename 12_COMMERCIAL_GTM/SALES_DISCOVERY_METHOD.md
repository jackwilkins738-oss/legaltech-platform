# SALES DISCOVERY METHOD

## Goal
Diagnose operational economics, not pitch features.

## Opening
Understand:
- firm
- practice areas
- Proclaim setup
- team size
- matter volume
- admin structure

## Workflow questions
- Which inboxes drive work?
- How are emails filed?
- How are attachments handled?
- How are tasks created?
- How are deadlines captured?
- What gets chased?
- What is still copied manually?
- What happens outside Proclaim?
- What causes backlogs?
- What causes rework?

## Change questions
- Who can change Proclaim workflows?
- How long does a change take?
- What does it cost?
- What requires external support?

## Commercial
- Proclaim users
- annual system cost where customer will share
- admin headcount
- planned hiring
- biggest operational constraint

## Security
- cloud policy
- AI policy
- DPO
- ISO/SOC expectations
- UK hosting
- procurement

## Buying
- budget owner
- champion
- security approver
- procurement
- timeline
- competing initiatives

## Qualification
Strong opportunity:
- frequent high-cost workflow
- measurable baseline
- buyer owns cost/problem
- customer will give controlled technical access
- willing to pilot

## Bad opportunity
- only wants generic AI demo
- no measurable pain
- no buyer
- expects free bespoke development
