# GO-TO-MARKET AND SALES

## Category positioning
Do not lead with:
"AI-powered legal case management."

Lead with measurable operational pain.

## Example direction
"Reduce the manual administration between Outlook and your case-management system."

## Sales sequence
1. Target Proclaim firms
2. Discovery
3. Workflow assessment
4. Quantified baseline
5. Controlled demo using representative data
6. Security/IT review
7. Paid pilot
8. Measure ROI
9. Contract
10. Expand

## Proof required
- baseline time
- automation rate
- exception rate
- accuracy
- user adoption
- annual capacity value
- risk controls

## Enterprise objections
Prepare for:
- "We can configure Proclaim to do this."
- "Access will build this."
- "We are moving to Evo."
- "We cannot send client data to AI."
- "Migration is too risky."
- "Our IT team won't permit it."
- "We don't want another system."
- "We already use Microsoft Copilot."
- "We cannot rely on a startup."

## Required sales assets
- ROI calculator
- security pack
- architecture diagram
- data-flow diagram
- subprocessor list
- DPA
- pilot scope
- case study
- Proclaim comparison
- integration explanation
- AI data-use statement
