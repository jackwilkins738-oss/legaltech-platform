# DAY-1 ANALYSIS RULES

## First analysis trigger
Approximately 30 observations OR the end of the first useful observation block.

## Order
1. Run `TO Quality Checks`.
2. Exclude/flag contaminated timings.
3. Review sample coverage by Session/Role/Sample Mode.
4. Rank observed categories by direct active minutes/events only.
5. Define specific Task Families.
6. Add/validate volume data separately.
7. Review Task Family × Role analysis.
8. Compare findings with pre-registered hypotheses.
9. Record strongest contradictory evidence.
10. Choose next observation needed.

## Do not
- call a final wedge from 30 events;
- quote annual savings based on unvalidated frequency;
- mix roles into one average without reason;
- treat a targeted repeat sample as prevalence;
- hide evidence that Proclaim already solves a problem.

## Output after Day 1
- top observed workload families;
- top cycle-time/external-dependency families;
- top 3 provisional wedge candidates;
- evidence confidence for each;
- missing data;
- next observation/interview/technical test.
