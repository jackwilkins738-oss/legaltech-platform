# MEASUREMENT AND ANNUALISATION RULES

## Three different evidence types
Never collapse these into one confidence label.

### Timing
How long one event takes.
Preferred evidence: direct measured observation.

### Frequency
How many such events occur per person/day/week.
Preferred evidence:
1. system/report data;
2. sampled count across representative periods;
3. direct user report;
4. assumption.

### Population
How many people actually perform the task.
Preferred evidence:
1. roster/system data;
2. manager confirmation;
3. user report;
4. assumption.

## Analysis unit
`Task Family × Role`, not broad Task Category.

Example:
- `File insurer liability email × Legal Admin`
- `File medical report × File Handler`

Do not average them together merely because both are `FILE`.

## Annual capacity formula
For a validated row:

`events/person/day × people × working days × (avg active minutes + avg review minutes) / 60 × loaded hourly cost`

## Evidence gate
Provisional annual ranking requires:
- >=5 included measured timings for the Task Family × Role;
- volume row marked Validated = Yes;
- frequency source not merely repeated from observation rows;
- role population source recorded.

## Capacity is not cash
Label output `capacity value`, not cash saving, unless there is evidence of:
- avoided hire;
- overtime/contractor reduction;
- increased profitable case throughput;
- other realised financial impact.

## Waiting
Track waiting as cycle-time evidence separately.
Do not multiply multi-day waiting by hourly labour cost.
