# EMPLOYEE AND CLIENT PRIVACY GUARDRAILS

## Authorisation
Confirm the firm/appropriate manager has authorised the process observation before starting.

## Purpose limitation
The study is for workflow/product research, not employee performance monitoring.

Do not record:
- employee names unless genuinely necessary and authorised;
- productivity rankings by named employee;
- client-identifiable facts;
- privileged correspondence content;
- medical details;
- bank/payment details.

## Recording
No covert audio, video or screen recording.
Any recording/screenshot requires explicit firm approval and an approved storage/data-handling path.

## Anonymisation
Use:
- roles, not names;
- MAT-001 style matter aliases;
- generic external-party labels;
- generic document types.

## Sharing with AI tools
Do not paste customer/client confidential content into general-purpose AI chats.
Use anonymised/process-only data unless an approved customer data-processing arrangement explicitly permits otherwise.
