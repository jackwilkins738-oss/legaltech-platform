# OBSERVER CALIBRATION

## Before measured sampling
Run 3 calibration events.

For each, verify the observer can consistently answer:
1. What triggered the task?
2. Where does the task start/end?
3. What is active handling vs waiting?
4. What is the task category?
5. What is the more-specific Task Family?
6. Was it normal or an exception?
7. Was timing interrupted?
8. What systems were actually touched?
9. Was legal judgement required?
10. Could native Proclaim already automate all/part?

## Task boundary rule
Create a new observation when there is a meaningful new purpose/output or a substantial wait/handoff.

Do not split every click into a task.
Do not combine an entire matter into one task.

## Multiple observers
If more than one observer is used, double-code at least 5 shared/example events and reconcile differences before combining data.

## Observer influence
If the employee noticeably changes behaviour because they are being observed, note it in Session Log / Notes.
