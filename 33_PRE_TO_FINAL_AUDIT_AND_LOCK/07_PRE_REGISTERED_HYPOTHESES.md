# PRE-REGISTERED TO HYPOTHESES

These are recorded before TO evidence lands to reduce confirmation bias.

## H1 — inbound handling
Inbound email/document handling will be a top operational burden.

Kills/weakens:
low frequency/time; strong native automation; very low review burden.

## H2 — matter matching
Matter matching/search creates meaningful repeated friction.

Kills/weakens:
references are nearly always exact and matching takes negligible time.

## H3 — downstream action creation
Inbound communications frequently cause separate manual task/action creation.

Kills/weakens:
rare, already automatic, or mostly legal judgement.

## H4 — external dependency work
Chasing/evidence/records may be a larger value pool than filing.

Supports:
repeated touches, long waits, client/cash/cycle-time consequences.

## H5 — supervisor review
Team leaders spend material time identifying stale/missing work.

Kills/weakens:
low frequency/time or reliable existing Proclaim reporting/workflows.

## H6 — review economics
Human review will not consume most of the theoretical automation saving.

Kills/weakens:
high ambiguity, high exception rate, long review time.

## H7 — augmentation gap
The core pain cannot be solved cheaply/reliably by ordinary native Proclaim configuration alone.

Kills/weakens:
strong internal Proclaim users demonstrate an easy native solution.

## Rule
Do not edit a hypothesis to make the results look supportive. Mark SUPPORTED, WEAKENED, REJECTED or MIXED and create a new hypothesis if needed.
