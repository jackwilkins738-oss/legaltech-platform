# POST-TO HANDOFF

## What to provide for analysis
- completed active TO workbook;
- anonymised observer notes;
- any approved aggregate/system counts;
- Proclaim environment questionnaire answers;
- clear note of abnormal sessions or gaps.

## Do not provide
- client-identifying information;
- raw confidential correspondence;
- medical records;
- passwords/API secrets;
- screenshots with unredacted client data.

## First post-TO outputs
1. data-quality report;
2. observed workload analysis;
3. Task Family × Role economics with evidence labels;
4. exception/judgement analysis;
5. native-Proclaim/configuration challenge;
6. top three wedge candidates;
7. next evidence required;
8. build/stop/reorder recommendation.
