## Do not use as the active TO workbook
- `LegalTech_Pre_TO_Decision_Workbook.xlsx`
- `LegalTech_Pre_TO_Execution_Workbook.xlsx`

They are historical/supporting workbooks. The Day-0 workbook supersedes them for observation capture.

## Before observation begins
All must be true:
- observation authorised by the firm/appropriate manager;
- observer understands anonymisation;
- first session logged;
- 3 calibration events completed;
- sampling starts with a continuous block where practical;
- pre-registered hypotheses remain unchanged until first analysis.

## During observation
Capture process, not employee performance.
Do not copy client-identifiable or privileged content.
Do not select only workflows that support the current inbound thesis.

## After the first block
Do not choose a wedge from raw event counts.
Run the data-quality gate, separate timing from volume, then identify what evidence is still missing.


## Deep-audit supplement
Before drawing conclusions from the TO, also use `35_PRE_TO_DEEP_AUDIT/`. The active observation workbook remains `25_TO_DAY0_OPERATING_KIT/LegalTech_TO_Day0_Execution_Workbook.xlsx`; the deep-audit workbook is a supplement for coverage, root-cause, Proclaim maturity, shadow systems, baseline KPIs and limitations.
