# VOLUME VALIDATION PROTOCOL

## Why
Frequency is the largest multiplier in annual ROI. Weak frequency assumptions can overwhelm accurate timing.

## For each high-potential Task Family × Role
Seek one of:
1. Proclaim/report count;
2. mailbox/document count;
3. team management report;
4. sample count over representative days;
5. manager/user estimate if nothing better exists.

## Record
- events/person/day;
- number of people;
- working days;
- source;
- source detail;
- confidence;
- validated yes/no.

## Cross-check
If a user says 20/day but the observed continuous block implies 3/day, do not average them. Investigate the mismatch.

## Minimum rule
No annual £ figure is decision-grade while `Validated? = No`.

An assumption may still be used for sensitivity modelling, but must remain labelled ASSUMPTION.
