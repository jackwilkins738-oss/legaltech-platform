# PERSONA-SPECIFIC OUTREACH DRAFTS

## Proclaim development lead
Subject: Research on what still sits outside Proclaim

We're researching operational work around Proclaim in claimant teams and specifically trying to separate genuine platform limitations from configuration/resource issues. Your background running Proclaim development makes you exactly the sort of person who could disprove weak assumptions quickly. Would you be open to a 20-minute research call on what Proclaim already does well, what still ends up in Outlook/manual handling, and which changes are disproportionately difficult to maintain?

## Operations leader
Subject: Claims operations research

We're studying where administrative work and external waiting still slow claimant matters even in well-run firms. The focus is practical: inbound correspondence, evidence, chasing, task creation, client updates and matter progression around existing case-management systems. Would you be open to a short research conversation on which workflows genuinely consume capacity or delay cases? We are not asking you to replace your PMS.

## Managing director / economic buyer
Subject: PI workflow research

We're researching whether specific operational work around existing case-management systems is expensive enough to justify a narrowly scoped automation pilot. Rather than pitching a generic AI product, we're trying to understand which repeated workflows actually consume headcount, slow settlements or create supervision burden. Could I ask you 20 minutes of questions about how you would evaluate that economically?

## IT/security lead
Subject: Controlled integration research

We're testing the feasibility of an augmentation layer that keeps the existing PMS as the source of record. Before building around assumptions, we're speaking to law-firm IT teams about whether tightly scoped Microsoft Graph and PMS integration would be acceptable, and what security/procurement conditions would apply. Would you be open to a short technical research call?
