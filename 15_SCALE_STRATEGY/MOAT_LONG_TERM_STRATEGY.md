# MOAT AND LONG-TERM STRATEGY

## Weak moats
- prettier UI
- ChatGPT wrapper
- basic summaries
- generic workflow builder
- cloud hosting
- basic dashboards

## Stronger potential moats
### Workflow intelligence
Large dataset of which operational patterns work across specific legal practices.

### Migration technology
Repeatable Proclaim discovery, mapping, reconciliation and cutover.

### Integration depth
Reliable deep connectors into the systems law firms actually use.

### Practice-area operating models
Prebuilt, configurable high-quality workflows.

### Automation evaluation data
Known accuracy/exception rates for specific legal-admin tasks.

### Trust
Security certifications, procurement readiness, strong track record.

### Embeddedness
System becomes the operational execution layer across the firm.

## End-state possibility
The product starts as an automation/operations layer.
Over time:
- users spend more time in it
- canonical data expands
- workflows move into it
- reporting moves into it
- documents/communications become native
Eventually Proclaim becomes the dependency rather than the interface.
Then replacement is rational.
