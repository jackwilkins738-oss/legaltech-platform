# COMPETITIVE WHITE SPACE & MOAT

## White space
The market is crowded in PMS, legal AI, research and portals. The less-solved combination is:

1. Works alongside Proclaim immediately, with no risky replacement.
2. Automates operations aggressively while remaining conservative around legal judgment.
3. Exception-first UX rather than another giant task list.
4. UK volume-litigation depth rather than generic legal software or US-only PI assumptions.
5. Migration built into the architecture from day one.
6. ROI instrumentation on every automation.
7. Customer-owned configuration without specialist-developer dependency.

## Moat stack
1. Proclaim integration knowledge.
2. Canonical legal-operations data model.
3. Workflow/event dataset.
4. PI practice packs.
5. Firm-specific skills/workflows.
6. Automation evaluation datasets.
7. Migration/reconciliation engine.
8. Embedded integrations.
9. Operational benchmarks across firms.
10. Enterprise security/trust posture.

## Biggest threat
Incumbents can copy AI UI quickly.

They have more difficulty copying:
- better migration,
- deeper operational benchmarks,
- exception-first working,
- reusable vertical workflows,
- customer-owned configuration,
- measurable automation economics.

## Product rule
Build only if it does at least one:
- removes repeated human action,
- reduces operational/legal risk,
- increases throughput,
- reduces time-to-resolution,
- measurably improves client service,
- lowers migration/switching risk,
- deepens canonical matter context.
