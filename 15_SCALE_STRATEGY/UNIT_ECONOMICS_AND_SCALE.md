# UNIT ECONOMICS AND SCALE

## Model stages
- 1 firm
- 10 firms
- 100 firms
- 1,000 firms

At each stage model:
- ARR
- user count
- matters
- emails/day
- documents/day
- storage
- OCR
- AI calls
- DB load
- search load
- support tickets
- onboarding team
- security/compliance spend
- SRE
- sales

- ACV
- implementation revenue
- gross margin
- cloud COGS
- AI COGS
- support COGS
- onboarding cost
- CAC
- CAC payback
- NRR
- logo retention
- expansion revenue
- deployment time
- time-to-value

## Scale principle
Enterprise SaaS becomes dangerous when:
revenue scales linearly but implementation/support scales almost as fast.
Productise configuration and migration early.
