# REGULATORY & PROCUREMENT BASELINE

## SRA confidentiality
SRA guidance states:
- current and former client affairs must be confidential unless disclosure is legally permitted/required or consented to
- confidentiality is broader than simple non-disclosure
- outsourcing/cloud requires adequate protection
- information barriers may be required where adverse interests/confidential information exist
- privileged information must be distinguished from ordinary confidential information

## Product consequences
Must support:
- matter-level security
- restricted matters
- restricted documents
- information barriers
- conflict quarantine
- controlled export/share
- auditing
- approval before material external disclosure
- subprocessor transparency

## UK GDPR
Likely law-firm datasets include special-category data.

ICO guidance:
- Article 6 lawful basis is still needed
- Article 9 condition is needed for special-category data
- large-scale special-category processing can require DPIA
- processors have direct legal obligations
- processor/controller status depends on actual processing role, not contract labels alone

## 2026 regulatory freshness
ICO guidance currently notes it is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act.
Therefore compliance files must carry "recheck date" and not hard-code regulatory guidance indefinitely.
