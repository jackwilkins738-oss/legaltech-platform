# ISO EVIDENCE MAP

## Principle
Certification is evidence that a management system operates.
Policies alone are insufficient.

## ISO 27001:2022 evidence classes
For each applicable control maintain:

### 1. Requirement
What needs to happen?

### 2. Policy
What company says must happen.

### 3. Procedure
How it happens.

### 4. Technical control
Code/config/service enforcing it.

### 5. Operational evidence
Examples:
- access-review records
- tickets
- scan reports
- training records
- incident exercises
- backup restore result

### 6. Test
How we prove control is effective.

### 7. Exception
Approved deviation.

### 8. Corrective action
How issue is fixed.

### 9. Owner
Named role.

## ISO 27701:2025
Privacy Information Management System:
- PII inventory
- controller/processor roles
- purposes
- legal bases/conditions
- rights
- retention
- processors
- transfers
- privacy risk
- privacy incidents
- privacy-by-design
- PIMS review

## ISO 42001:2023
AI Management System:
- AI inventory
- context/scope
- AI policy
- AI risk assessment
- impact assessment
- provider governance
- data governance
- transparency
- human oversight
- monitoring
- incident handling
- continual improvement

## Evidence repository structure
/compliance/evidence/YYYY/MM/
Do not place secrets or unnecessary client data into compliance evidence.
