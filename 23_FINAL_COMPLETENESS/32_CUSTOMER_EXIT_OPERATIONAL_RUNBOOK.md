# CUSTOMER EXIT OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK

## Trigger
- termination;
- failed pilot;
- customer migration;
- insolvency/closure as contract allows.

## Actions
1. confirm contractual dates;
2. stop new scope/automations;
3. produce export;
4. validate counts/hashes;
5. customer receipt;
6. revoke Graph/Proclaim tokens;
7. disable users;
8. remove support grants;
9. active-data deletion;
10. backup expiry tracking;
11. vendor/subprocessor deletion where applicable;
12. closure confirmation.

## Preserve
- minimum legal/accounting contract records;
- security/incident evidence;
- audit evidence as required.

Do not preserve customer matter content indefinitely "just in case."

## Exit success
Customer can reconstruct/export its records without vendor lock-in.
