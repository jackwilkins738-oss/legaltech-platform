# FOUNDER RACI & OPERATING CADENCE

## Two-founder default

### Founder A — Product / Commercial
Accountable:
- customer research;
- ICP;
- sales;
- pricing;
- product requirements;
- pilot value;
- finance/admin.

### Founder B — Technical
Accountable:
- architecture;
- engineering;
- integrations;
- infrastructure;
- security implementation;
- technical support.

### Shared decisions
Require both:
- equity/fundraising;
- annual commitments above agreed threshold;
- production-data access policy;
- material security exceptions;
- legal-accounting expansion;
- hiring;
- customer liability outside standard terms.

## Named owners even when only two people
- Security owner
- Privacy owner
- Incident commander
- Finance owner
- Vendor owner
- Customer success owner

One person can hold multiple roles, but ownership cannot be blank.

## Weekly operating meeting — 60 minutes
1. KPI scorecard — 10m
2. Evidence learned — 10m
3. Product/engineering — 15m
4. Pipeline/customers — 10m
5. Security/risks — 5m
6. Cash/runway — 5m
7. Decisions/owners — 5m

## Daily founder check — 10 minutes
- yesterday;
- today;
- blocker;
- customer/security incident?

## Monthly
- accounts/runway;
- vendor cost;
- risk register;
- access review;
- backup evidence;
- roadmap;
- customer health.

## Decision hygiene
Every material decision records:
- decision;
- evidence;
- owner;
- date;
- revisit trigger.
