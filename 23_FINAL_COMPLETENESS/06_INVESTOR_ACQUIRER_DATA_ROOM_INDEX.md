# INVESTOR / ACQUIRER DATA ROOM INDEX

## Corporate
- certificate/incorporation;
- articles;
- cap table;
- shareholder/founder agreements;
- board/shareholder resolutions.

## IP
- founder IP assignments;
- employee/contractor assignments;
- trademarks/domains;
- OSS policy/SBOM.

## Financial
- accounts;
- management accounts;
- bank;
- tax;
- forecasts;
- R&D claims.

## Customers
- signed contracts;
- pipeline summary;
- ARR/ACV;
- churn;
- concentration;
- pilot results.

## Product
- architecture;
- roadmap;
- product analytics;
- AI inventory;
- evaluation results.

## Security/privacy
- policies;
- risk register;
- pen test;
- incidents;
- ISO/CE evidence;
- DPIAs;
- ROPA;
- subprocessors;
- DPA templates.

## People
- employees;
- options;
- contractors;
- invention/IP clauses.

## Legal/regulatory
- material disputes;
- insurance;
- regulatory correspondence;
- standard customer terms.

## Technical diligence
- repo ownership;
- CI/CD;
- dependency/SBOM;
- backup/DR;
- SLO/incidents.

## Rule
Maintain continuously. Do not scramble to manufacture diligence after a term sheet.
