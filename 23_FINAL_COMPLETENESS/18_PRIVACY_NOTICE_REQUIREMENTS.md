# PRIVACY NOTICE REQUIREMENTS

ICO guidance says privacy information generally needs to cover identity/contact details, purposes, lawful basis, recipients, transfers and other required information depending on the context.

Source:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/

## Notices potentially required
- company website;
- sales/prospects;
- customer admins/users;
- recruitment;
- support;
- cookies/analytics;
- product processing explanation where appropriate.

## Product users
Explain:
- company role;
- customer/law-firm role;
- categories processed;
- product telemetry;
- subprocessors;
- AI;
- transfers;
- retention;
- rights/contact.

## Avoid
A single vague notice claiming every possible lawful basis.

## Change
Version and effective date.
Material change communicated where appropriate.

Because ICO guidance is being updated following the Data (Use and Access) Act, counsel/privacy review should confirm launch wording.
