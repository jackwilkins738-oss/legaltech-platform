# COMPLIANCE / CORPORATE CALENDAR

## Monthly
- access anomalies;
- vendor/security incidents;
- backup success;
- security risk changes;
- cash/runway.

## Quarterly
- access review;
- restore drill;
- incident tabletop;
- critical vendor review/change check;
- policy exceptions;
- AI inventory/model review;
- customer subprocessors.

## Annual
- Companies House filings/confirmation statement;
- accounts/tax;
- ICO fee renewal if applicable;
- insurance;
- Cyber Essentials renewal once held;
- pen test;
- policy review;
- DPIA/ROPA review;
- business continuity exercise;
- staff security/privacy training.

## Event-driven
- new subprocessor;
- new country;
- new high-risk AI feature;
- acquisition/fundraise;
- security incident;
- new regulated workflow;
- material architecture change.

Assign owners/dates in company calendar; this file is only the control list.
