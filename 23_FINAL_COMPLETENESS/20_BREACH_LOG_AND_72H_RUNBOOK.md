# PERSONAL DATA BREACH LOG / 72-HOUR RUNBOOK

ICO requires organisations to keep records of personal data breaches, including those not notified. Reportable UK GDPR breaches must be notified without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness.

Source:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/personal-data-breach/personal-data-breaches-a-guide/

## Minute 0–60
- open incident;
- preserve evidence;
- contain;
- incident commander;
- privacy lead;
- identify customer/controller.

## Hours 1–8
Assess:
- what data;
- how many people/records;
- special category;
- confidentiality/integrity/availability;
- attacker/access;
- containment;
- likely risk to individuals.

## Hours 8–24
- customer notification under contract/DPA;
- decide controller/processor responsibilities;
- legal/privacy review;
- draft regulator/individual communication if relevant.

## By 72 hours
Where we are controller and threshold met:
notify ICO even if investigation incomplete; supplement later.

Where customer is controller:
provide facts promptly enough for customer to meet its duties.

## Breach log fields
- incident ID;
- awareness time;
- event time;
- nature;
- data;
- people/records;
- risk;
- containment;
- notifications;
- rationale if not reported;
- remediation;
- closure.

## High-risk individuals
If high risk to rights/freedoms, controller may need to communicate without undue delay.
