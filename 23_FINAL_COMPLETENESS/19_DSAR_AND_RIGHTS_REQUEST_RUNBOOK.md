# DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS / SAR RUNBOOK

## Intake
Requests can arrive through ordinary channels; do not require magic wording.

Record:
- received date;
- requester;
- right;
- identity/auth;
- customer/controller relevance;
- scope;
- deadline.

## Processor scenario
If request concerns customer matter data and customer is controller:
- notify/route according to DPA;
- do not independently decide disclosure unless authorised/required.

## SAR timing
ICO guidance says respond without undue delay and generally within one month, subject to current rules and permitted extensions/clarification.

Source:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-of-access/what-should-we-consider-when-responding-to-a-request/

## Search sources
- account;
- CRM;
- support;
- audit where personal;
- production/customer data as instructed;
- logs;
- HR/recruitment.

## Review
- third-party rights;
- privilege/exemptions;
- security/identity;
- customer instructions.

## Evidence
Retain request log, searches, decisions and response date.
