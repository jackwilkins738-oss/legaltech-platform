# ROPA / DATA PROCESSING INVENTORY

## Maintain one row per processing activity

Fields:
- activity;
- owner;
- controller/processor role;
- purposes;
- data subjects;
- categories;
- special-category/criminal data;
- lawful basis where controller;
- Article 9 condition where applicable;
- recipients;
- subprocessors;
- countries/regions;
- transfer mechanism;
- retention;
- security controls;
- DPIA required?;
- deletion method.

## Initial company activities
- customer account administration;
- product legal-matter processing;
- support;
- security logging;
- billing;
- sales CRM;
- recruitment;
- staff administration;
- website analytics;
- product telemetry;
- AI provider processing.

## Rule
Customer matter processing should not be collapsed into marketing/product analytics.

Different purpose, role and permissions.
