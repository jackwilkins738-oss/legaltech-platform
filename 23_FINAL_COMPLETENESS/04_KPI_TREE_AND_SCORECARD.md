# KPI TREE & COMPANY SCORECARD

## North-star principle
Do not optimise for prompts, documents processed or AI calls.
Optimise for **safe legal work progressed with less human administration**.

## Product/value KPIs
- human minutes/event before vs after;
- annualised capacity released;
- auto-action rate;
- review rate;
- correction rate;
- exception age;
- matter progression time;
- client-update SLA compliance.

## Safety
- wrong-client filing incidents;
- material wrong-matter incidents;
- cross-tenant events;
- high-impact AI errors;
- security incidents;
- failed audit events.

Any cross-tenant confidentiality event is critical regardless of growth.

## Adoption
- WAU / intended users;
- actions/user/week;
- queue completion;
- workflow bypass rate;
- time-to-first-value;
- training completion.

## Reliability
- availability;
- processing latency;
- Graph backlog;
- Proclaim writeback success;
- backup success;
- restore-test status.

## Commercial
- qualified pipeline;
- pilots proposed;
- paid pilots;
- pilot→annual conversion;
- ARR;
- ACV;
- sales cycle;
- expansion ARR;
- churn/NRR when meaningful.

## Unit economics
- implementation hours/customer;
- support hours/customer;
- AI/OCR COGS;
- gross margin;
- CAC later;
- payback later.

## Company
- cash;
- monthly burn;
- runway;
- headcount;
- critical-role vacancy.

## Stage discipline
Pre-revenue:
evidence + pilot pipeline > vanity ARR forecasts.

Pilot stage:
measured value + adoption + safety > user counts.

Scale:
ARR/NRR/gross margin + reliability + security.
