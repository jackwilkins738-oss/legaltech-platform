# DPIA TEMPLATE

## 1. Processing
- feature/workflow;
- controller/processor roles;
- purpose;
- data flow;
- systems/providers.

## 2. Data
- personal data;
- special category;
- criminal-offence data;
- children/vulnerable people;
- volumes;
- retention.

## 3. Necessity/proportionality
- objective;
- why needed;
- minimisation;
- alternatives;
- access.

## 4. Individuals
- claimant/client;
- opponent;
- witnesses;
- staff;
- medical professionals;
- others.

## 5. Risks
Score likelihood/impact:
- wrong-matter disclosure;
- cross-tenant;
- model hallucination;
- prompt injection;
- excessive access;
- provider transfer;
- automated significant decision;
- retention;
- re-identification.

## 6. Controls
- human approval;
- tenant isolation;
- information barriers;
- encryption;
- audit;
- minimisation;
- eval;
- retention;
- provider contracts.

## 7. Residual risk
LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH.

If high residual risk remains, obtain privacy/legal advice and determine whether prior consultation with ICO is required.

## 8. Approval
- product;
- security;
- privacy;
- customer/controller where relevant;
- date;
- review trigger.
