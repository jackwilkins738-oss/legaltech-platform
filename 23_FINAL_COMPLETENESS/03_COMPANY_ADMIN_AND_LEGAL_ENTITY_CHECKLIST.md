# COMPANY ADMIN / LEGAL ENTITY CHECKLIST

## Incorporation
- UK Ltd incorporated;
- registered office;
- SIC codes reviewed;
- statutory registers/company records;
- business bank;
- accounting system.

## Companies House identity
Compulsory identity verification for directors and PSCs came into effect from 18 November 2025. Verify all required founders/directors/PSCs and maintain personal codes/process evidence.

Sources:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/when-you-need-to-verify-your-identity-for-companies-house
https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/companies-house-management-information-identity-verification-april-to-june-2026

## Founder/IP
- founder agreement/shareholder agreement;
- vesting/reverse vesting where appropriate;
- IP assignment to company;
- contractor IP/confidentiality agreements;
- domains/company accounts company-owned.

## Data protection
ICO says organisations using personal information may need to pay the annual data-protection fee unless exempt. Perform the official self-assessment and register/pay if required.

Source:
https://ico.org.uk/fee

## Insurance
Before customer pilot:
- tech E&O / professional indemnity;
- cyber;
- employers' liability if required;
- public liability as appropriate.

## Tax/accounting
- Corporation Tax;
- PAYE if hiring;
- VAT threshold/strategy reviewed;
- R&D records where relevant;
- monthly management accounts.

## Legal review before production
UK technology/privacy counsel:
- MSA;
- Order Form;
- DPA;
- Security Schedule;
- AI terms;
- SLA/support;
- liability.
