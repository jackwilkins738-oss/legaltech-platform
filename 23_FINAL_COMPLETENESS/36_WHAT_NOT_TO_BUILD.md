# WHAT NOT TO BUILD YET

Until evidence changes the decision:

- full legal accounting;
- client-money ledger;
- generic legal research database;
- autonomous legal advice;
- autonomous court filing;
- full PMS replacement;
- international practice packs;
- giant integration marketplace;
- voice infrastructure;
- native e-signature cryptography;
- payment processor;
- dozens of dashboards;
- consumer mobile app;
- broad CRM;
- arbitrary-code workflow engine;
- unrestricted autonomous email sending.

## Why
Each either:
- adds regulation/risk;
- is already a mature specialist market;
- does not prove the wedge;
- expands scope before repeatability.

## Exception
A real paid customer can reopen a decision, but only through documented product/economic/security analysis.
