# CUSTOMER CONFIGURATION HANDOVER

## Customer configuration pack
At go-live produce:
- tenant ID;
- enabled modules;
- mailboxes;
- role mapping;
- security zones;
- practice packs;
- Proclaim mappings;
- workflows + versions;
- automation policy;
- AI/model policy;
- retention;
- integrations;
- escalation contacts.

## Customer approval
Customer signs off:
- which workflows may auto-run;
- which require approval;
- who approves;
- data regions/subprocessors;
- support access;
- retention.

## Change control
After go-live:
- request;
- risk;
- test;
- approver;
- effective date;
- rollback.

No undocumented "quick fixes" in production configuration.
