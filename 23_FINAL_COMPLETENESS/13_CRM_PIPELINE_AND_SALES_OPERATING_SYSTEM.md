# CRM / SALES OPERATING SYSTEM

## Account stages
1. RESEARCHED
2. CONTACTED
3. ENGAGED
4. DISCOVERY
5. QUALIFIED
6. WORKFLOW ASSESSMENT
7. SECURITY/TECHNICAL
8. PILOT PROPOSAL
9. PILOT CONTRACTING
10. PILOT LIVE
11. ANNUAL NEGOTIATION
12. WON / LOST

## Required qualification
- Proclaim/current PMS evidence;
- workflow pain;
- volume;
- buyer;
- technical access plausibility;
- urgency;
- measurable outcome.

## Opportunity fields
- practice;
- users;
- workflow;
- annual value estimate;
- champion;
- economic buyer;
- IT owner;
- security owner;
- next step/date;
- blocker;
- loss reason.

## Pipeline review weekly
Ask only:
- what changed?
- next customer action?
- next our action?
- evidence of pain?
- evidence of buying authority?
- close/pilot date?

## Disqualify
- wants free bespoke product;
- no measurable workflow;
- no access;
- no sponsor;
- generic AI curiosity only.

## Loss reasons
Keep structured:
- incumbent;
- no budget;
- no urgency;
- security;
- technical;
- Evo/migration;
- feature;
- startup risk;
- pricing;
- internal build.
