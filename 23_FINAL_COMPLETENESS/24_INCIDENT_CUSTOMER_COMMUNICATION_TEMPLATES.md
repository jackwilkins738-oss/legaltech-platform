# INCIDENT CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES

## Initial notice
- incident ID;
- detected time;
- affected service;
- known customer impact;
- what we have done;
- what customer should do;
- next update time.

Avoid speculation.

## Security/privacy notice
State:
- known facts;
- data/system affected;
- containment;
- whether unauthorised access confirmed;
- actions;
- customer contact;
- next update.

Do not prematurely say "no data was accessed" before evidence.

## Resolution
- start/end;
- root cause at appropriate level;
- customer impact;
- remediation;
- preventive actions;
- follow-up/RCA date.

## Status-page rule
Operational availability may be public.
Confidential security specifics may require direct customer communication.

## Internal approval
Incident commander + security/privacy + legal/comms as severity requires.
