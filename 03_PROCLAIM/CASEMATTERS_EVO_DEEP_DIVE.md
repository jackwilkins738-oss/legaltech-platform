# CASEMATTERS EVO DEEP DIVE

## Official positioning
Access currently markets CaseMatters Evo from the Proclaim Help Centre as:
- browser-based
- AI-assisted
- workflow/admin reduction
- substantially faster

A July 2026 Access newsletter says CaseMatters Evo combines:
- case management
- document handling
- time recording
in an "intelligent platform."

## Competitive threat
CaseMatters Evo directly attacks several obvious Proclaim weaknesses:
- desktop UX
- performance
- AI
- admin burden
- browser access

Therefore these are NOT sufficient differentiators for us.

## 2026 user-reported concerns
Unofficial Proclaim forum users report:
- uncertainty about whether movement becomes compulsory
- migration costs
- cloud-hosting control
- lack of clarity about full feature parity
- concern over AI permissions/control
- concern that some roles may still require desktop Proclaim
- early demo/polish concerns
- uncertainty about migration of existing customisation

These reports are anecdotal and must not be presented as official facts.

## Strategic response
Compete on areas harder to copy:
- migration certainty
- workflow equivalence testing
- measurable admin elimination
- exception-based operations
- open API
- controlled AI permissions
- transparent auditability
- easier configuration
- exportability
- pricing predictability
