# PROCLAIM RESEARCH MAP

Research each area as: verified capability, verified limitation, repeated complaint, workaround, open question, opportunity, competitive risk.

## Areas
1. Commercial: licence bands, hosting, modules, support, upgrades, services, integration costs, TCO.
2. Architecture: client/server, database, Windows/Office/Outlook, RDS, hosting, cloud/Evo direction, updates.
3. Core case management: matter creation, contacts, fields, stages, history, tasks, permissions, audit.
4. Workflow: triggers, conditions, tasks, documents, emails, chasing, approvals, configuration, versioning, testing.
5. Documents: storage, history, folders, versioning, search, metadata, OCR, import/export.
6. Email/Outlook: filing, matching, automated import, attachments, Outlook compatibility, Graph/API, failure modes, clicks.
7. Reporting/data: native reporting, SQL, exports, BI, dashboards, real-time reporting.
8. Accounting/time: time recording, billing, ledgers, client/office money, reconciliation.
9. API/integrations: REST/SOAP, auth, webhooks, sandbox, rate limits, documentation, 365, e-sign, AML/KYC, payments, telephony.
10. Support/configuration: response, changes, specialists, upgrades, consultancy dependence.
11. Migration/export: documents, metadata, workflows, users, audit, finance, practical exit difficulty.
12. CaseMatters Evo: official promises, AI, performance, browser, parity, migration path, complaints and rollout.
