# COMMUNICATIONS CHANNELS & PECR

## Channels eventually relevant
- email;
- SMS;
- WhatsApp/business messaging;
- voice;
- portal notifications;
- postal letters.

## Distinguish service from marketing
Case update/request:
usually operational service communication.

Cross-sell/promotion:
may be direct marketing.

PECR rules are stricter for marketing to individuals; corporate B2B email has different treatment. Sole traders/some partnerships can be treated like individuals.

ICO:
- https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/
- https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/

## Product model
Every outbound communication gets:
- purpose;
- matter;
- recipient;
- channel;
- template;
- approval policy;
- delivery state;
- opt-out relevance;
- audit.

## Channel policy
Do not allow a workflow to switch from service message to marketing merely because a template changed.

## SMS/WhatsApp
Treat providers as subprocessors.
Review:
- region;
- retention;
- delivery logs;
- phone-number exposure.

## Voice
Record/transcribe only with clear lawful-basis/transparency analysis and customer policy.
