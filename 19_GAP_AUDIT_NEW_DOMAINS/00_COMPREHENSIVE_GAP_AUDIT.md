# COMPREHENSIVE GAP AUDIT — OCTOBER 2026

## Purpose
Identify material areas a serious UK legal-tech company still needs to understand after reviewing the entire master repository.

## Conclusion
The repository is already unusually deep on:
- Proclaim
- V1 inbound product
- Microsoft Graph
- security/privacy
- AI safety/evaluation
- migration
- commercial model
- procurement basics
- support/SRE
- PI/clinical-negligence workflow
- competitor strategy
- TO research.

The remaining gaps are mostly **second-order execution risks** rather than obvious product features.

## Priority A — important before or during first pilot
1. Change management / user adoption.
2. Customer success, renewal and expansion.
3. QA, release engineering and regression strategy.
4. Performance, capacity and FinOps.
5. Data quality / schema evolution.
6. Customer export, exit and offboarding.
7. Accessibility / inclusive design.
8. SRA AI professional-duty requirements.
9. UK automated-decision rules after the Data (Use and Access) Act.
10. Product analytics and telemetry governance.
11. Document/precedent/bundle lifecycle.
12. Founder/company/IP ownership.

## Priority B — design now, build later
13. API/ecosystem governance.
14. EU AI Act readiness.
15. Electronic signatures.
16. Multi-channel communications / PECR.
17. HMCTS/court integration.
18. Medical-record request orchestration.
19. LLMOps / AI cost, latency and fallback engineering.
20. Open-source/software supply-chain licensing.
21. Knowledge/precedent governance.
22. Partner/channel strategy.

## Priority C — expansion readiness
23. Public-sector/G-Cloud procurement.
24. International/jurisdiction expansion.
25. Finance/tax/R&D-relief operating model.

## What is still intentionally blocked by evidence
No amount of desk research can determine:
- actual TO burden;
- real Proclaim production behaviour;
- buyer willingness to pay;
- real exception rate;
- real AI accuracy on client data.

This gap audit does **not** reopen those evidence-dependent questions.
