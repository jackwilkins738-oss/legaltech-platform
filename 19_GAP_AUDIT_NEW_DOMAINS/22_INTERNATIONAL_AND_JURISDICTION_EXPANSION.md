# INTERNATIONAL / JURISDICTION EXPANSION

## Architecture rule
Do not encode England-and-Wales legal rules into core workflow engine.

Keep jurisdiction-specific:
- court rules;
- limitation/deadline rules;
- document templates;
- terminology;
- regulatory controls;
- retention;
- legal research.

## Expansion order
1. England & Wales.
2. Scotland only after independent workflow/legal mapping.
3. Ireland/EU only after privacy/AI/court analysis.
4. US only as separate PI/product strategy.

## Tenant metadata
- jurisdiction;
- regulatory regime;
- data region;
- practice pack;
- locale/time zone;
- currency;
- court calendar.

## Data transfer
Cross-border customer expansion requires transfer-mechanism/subprocessor review.

## Commercial warning
Do not internationalise to chase TAM before UK repeatability.
