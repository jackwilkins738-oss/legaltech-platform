# ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSIVE DESIGN

## Target
WCAG 2.2 AA as product baseline.

GOV.UK requires WCAG 2.2 AA for public-sector web/mobile services and emphasises accessibility from project inception.

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/meet-the-requirements-of-equality-and-accessibility-regulations

## Why commercial legal SaaS should care
- disabled employees are users;
- public-sector procurement can demand evidence;
- accessibility improves keyboard-heavy legal workflows;
- Equality Act risk/customer policy requirements.

## V1 requirements
- keyboard complete;
- visible focus;
- no colour-only status;
- screen-reader labels;
- correct heading structure;
- accessible tables/forms;
- error summary + field errors;
- 200% zoom;
- high contrast;
- motion restraint;
- accessible modals/drawers.

## Legal-work specifics
Exception priority must not be only red/amber/green.
Documents need meaningful titles.
AI confidence must be text + number/explanation, not colour.

## Testing
- automated axe-style checks;
- keyboard manual test;
- NVDA/JAWS or VoiceOver test;
- zoom;
- contrast;
- user testing with disabled users before broad rollout.

## Deliverable
Accessibility statement once customer-facing maturity warrants it.
