# SRA AI PROFESSIONAL-DUTY REQUIREMENTS

## Current position
The SRA published a formal warning notice on misuse of AI on 17 August 2026.

It stresses:
- accountability remains with regulated professionals;
- AI inaccuracies/hallucinations can cause serious harm;
- confidentiality must be protected;
- appropriate supervision/human oversight is required;
- material used before courts must be checked.

Source:
https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/misuse-ai/

Law Society 2026 guidance similarly stresses human oversight and verification:
https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/contact-or-visit-us/press-office/press-releases/law-society-publishes-new-guide-warning-over-ai-risks

## Product consequences

### Court/legal submission
Never auto-submit AI-generated:
- legal authorities;
- pleadings;
- witness evidence;
- court forms
without explicit professional review.

### Legal research
If later integrated:
- authoritative source;
- jurisdiction/effective date;
- citation verification.

### Supervision
Audit:
- generated by AI;
- source;
- reviewer;
- approval;
- final output.

### Confidentiality
Firm must know:
- what leaves environment;
- provider;
- retention;
- training policy;
- region.

### User interface
High-impact output must state:
- generated/assisted;
- sources;
- verification status.

## Commercial implication
"Human in the loop" is too vague.
Specify which human, at which action, with what information.
