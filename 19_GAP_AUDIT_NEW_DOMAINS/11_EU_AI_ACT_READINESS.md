# EU AI ACT READINESS

## Why care
UK-only launch does not necessarily trigger EU AI Act, but selling to EU firms or deploying AI systems in EU can.

## Current timeline
EU AI Act transparency obligations under Article 50 apply from 2 August 2026.
Commission enforcement powers for GPAI-provider obligations also entered application on 2 August 2026.

Sources:
- https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems
- https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers

## Our likely role
If using OpenAI/Anthropic/etc:
we are generally a downstream provider/deployer of an AI-enabled legal SaaS, not the GPAI-model provider.

Role must be assessed per feature and territory.

## Readiness controls
Keep:
- AI system inventory;
- intended purpose;
- model/provider/version;
- human oversight;
- risk assessment;
- evaluation;
- incident log;
- transparency text;
- technical documentation;
- provider contracts.

## Avoid premature claim
Do not market product as "EU AI Act compliant" without a role/feature-specific legal assessment.

## Expansion gate
Before first EU customer:
commission EU AI Act mapping by counsel.
