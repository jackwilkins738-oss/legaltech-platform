# PARTNER / CHANNEL STRATEGY

## Potential partners
- Proclaim consultants/developers;
- legal IT MSPs;
- Microsoft partners;
- legal-sector consultancies;
- medical-record retrieval providers;
- e-sign/KYC/telephony vendors;
- migration specialists.

## Why
Partners can provide:
- distribution;
- implementation;
- customer trust;
- domain knowledge;
- API access knowledge.

## Risk
A channel can also:
- own customer relationship;
- demand margin;
- push bespoke work;
- become competitive.

## Partner tiers
Referral:
introduces lead.

Implementation:
certified configuration/deployment.

Technology:
API integration.

Strategic:
co-sell / joint proposition.

## Rules
- no exclusive channel early;
- retain direct customer relationship;
- standard implementation playbook;
- partner security requirements;
- margin model;
- certification;
- performance review.

## Proclaim ecosystem opportunity
Proclaim specialists may be allies rather than enemies.
Pitch:
"keep your Proclaim expertise; remove manual work around it."

A consultant could configure both systems.
