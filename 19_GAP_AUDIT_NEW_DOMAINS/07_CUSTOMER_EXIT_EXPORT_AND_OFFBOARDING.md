# CUSTOMER EXIT, EXPORT & OFFBOARDING

## Why it matters
Exportability reduces procurement fear and is a strategic contrast to legacy lock-in.

## Contract exit model
Define:
- export window;
- format;
- deletion schedule;
- legal hold exception;
- backup expiry;
- credential revocation;
- optional migration assistance.

## Export package
Machine-readable:
- matters;
- parties;
- tasks;
- communications metadata;
- document metadata;
- workflows/config;
- audit subset permitted by contract.

Binary:
- documents;
- document versions.

Manifest:
- counts;
- hashes;
- schema/version;
- mappings.

## Exit sequence
1. customer requests/termination occurs;
2. freeze new expansion;
3. produce validated export;
4. customer signs receipt where appropriate;
5. disconnect Graph/Proclaim;
6. disable users;
7. delete active data after agreed period;
8. expire backups according to documented schedule;
9. issue deletion confirmation.

## Data portability
UK GDPR portability rights apply only in specific controller/lawful-basis scenarios; do not confuse statutory individual portability with contractual customer export.

ICO:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-data-portability/

## Product principle
No proprietary binary format as the only export.
