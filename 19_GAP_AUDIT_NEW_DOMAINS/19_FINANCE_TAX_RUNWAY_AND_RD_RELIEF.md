## R&D relief
For accounting periods beginning on/after 1 April 2024, UK R&D relief moved to the merged RDEC scheme plus enhanced R&D intensive support for qualifying loss-making SMEs.

HMRC:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/research-and-development-rd-tax-relief-the-merged-scheme-and-enhanced-rd-intensive-support

Software work is not automatically qualifying R&D.
Use specialist tax advice and contemporaneous technical records.

## Technical evidence that may help
For qualifying projects, document:
- technological uncertainty;
- advance sought;
- competent professionals;
- experiments/failures;
- qualifying staff/contractor costs.
