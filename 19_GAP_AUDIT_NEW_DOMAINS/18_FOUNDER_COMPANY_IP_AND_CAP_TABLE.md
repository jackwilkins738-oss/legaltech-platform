# FOUNDER / COMPANY / IP STRUCTURE

## Why now
A future investor/acquirer will diligence chain of title, founder rights and contractor IP.

## Company
Private limited company by shares is typical.
Companies House model articles are defaults but may be insufficient for founder/investor arrangements.

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/model-articles-for-private-companies-limited-by-shares

## Founder agreement should address
- share ownership;
- vesting/reverse vesting;
- leaver treatment;
- decision rights;
- deadlock;
- founder roles;
- salaries;
- expenses;
- IP assignment;
- confidentiality;
- non-solicit where enforceable;
- what happens if one stops working;
- future financing.

Use UK startup counsel.

## IP
UK IPO says commissioned/freelance creators usually retain copyright unless contract transfers it; employee works created in employment are generally employer-owned.

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works

## Immediate requirement
Every contractor/freelancer:
- written IP assignment;
- confidentiality;
- open-source obligations;
- warranty they can transfer work.

Every founder:
assign pre-incorporation product code/design/domain/IP to company.

## Evidence folder
Maintain:
- incorporation docs;
- cap table;
- shareholder/founder agreement;
- IP assignments;
- contractor agreements;
- trademark/domain ownership.
