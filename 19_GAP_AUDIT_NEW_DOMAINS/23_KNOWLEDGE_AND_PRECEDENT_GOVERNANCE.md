# KNOWLEDGE / PRECEDENT GOVERNANCE

## Why separate from RAG
Legal knowledge has lifecycle and authority.

## Knowledge object
- title;
- type;
- jurisdiction;
- practice area;
- owner;
- approved by;
- effective from/to;
- source;
- version;
- confidentiality;
- superseded by.

## Types
- precedent;
- guidance note;
- checklist;
- policy;
- workflow instruction;
- court-rule note;
- client-specific instruction.

## RAG rule
Search should rank:
1. current approved;
2. correct jurisdiction;
3. correct practice;
4. customer-specific where applicable.

Never surface superseded precedent as equally authoritative.

## Review
Set:
- scheduled review;
- legal update trigger;
- owner;
- stale warning.

## AI
Model may summarise knowledge but must preserve source/version.
