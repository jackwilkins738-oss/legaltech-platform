## Procurement environment
The Procurement Act 2023 introduced additional transparency/performance requirements, with further provisions phased during 2026.

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/new-legislative-requirements-under-the-procurement-act-2023-html

## Public-sector expectations likely include
- accessibility WCAG 2.2 AA;
- Cyber Essentials;
- strong data location;
- DPIA/security evidence;
- sustainability/social value in some procurements;
- published pricing/service definition;
- exit plan.
