# CUSTOMER SUCCESS, RENEWAL & EXPANSION

## Problem
Enterprise SaaS can win a pilot and still lose the account if value is not visible after deployment.

## Customer lifecycle
1. qualified opportunity
2. pilot
3. production onboarding
4. adoption
5. value realisation
6. renewal
7. expansion
8. exit/offboarding

## Customer health score
Inputs:
- active users;
- workflow volume;
- automation rate;
- exception age;
- support burden;
- measurable hours removed;
- executive sponsor engagement;
- security/integration health;
- unresolved product gaps.

Do not create opaque "AI health score".
Every factor should be inspectable.

## QBR / value review
Quarterly for enterprise:
- baseline vs current;
- work removed;
- capacity/revenue impact;
- top exception causes;
- workflow changes;
- risk/incidents;
- roadmap;
- expansion candidates.

## Renewal risk signals
- usage falling;
- champions leave;
- Proclaim/Evo strategy changes;
- unresolved integration failures;
- high human review;
- support tickets ageing;
- no executive-visible ROI.

## Expansion ladder
Land:
Inbound operations.

Expand:
- more mailboxes;
- more teams;
- Matter Command;
- evidence completeness;
- records;
- client updates;
- practice packs;
- migration.

## Commercial rule
Expansion must follow demonstrated value, not feature pressure.

## CS staffing trigger
Founder-led until:
- onboarding becomes repeatable;
- 5–10 active customers;
- support/adoption work begins stealing product/sales time.

First CS hire should understand legal operations, not generic SaaS only.
