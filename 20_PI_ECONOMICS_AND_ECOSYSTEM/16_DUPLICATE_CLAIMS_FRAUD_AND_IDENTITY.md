# DUPLICATE CLAIMS, FRAUD & IDENTITY

## Current regulatory signal
SRA's 2026 claims-management guidance stresses checks to identify whether clients have already instructed someone else and prompt action where duplicate claims are found.

## Product opportunity
**Duplicate / Conflict Detection**
Signals:
- same name + DOB;
- same accident;
- same vehicle;
- same claim ref;
- same defendant;
- duplicate documents;
- known prior instruction.

## Guardrail
Do not label a client 'fraudulent' from probabilistic signals.

Output:
- possible duplicate;
- reason;
- human review.

## Identity
For high-volume remote intake:
- identity verification;
- client contact ownership;
- consent;
- bank-details change controls.

## Fraud-adjacent safety
Any fraud/high-risk scoring that affects representation can become a significant decision and requires human/legal review.

## Security
Bank detail changes should have strong verification to prevent payment diversion.
