# COSTS RECOVERY & FILE CLOSURE

## Matter is not economically finished at settlement
Possible remaining work:
- defendant costs payment;
- disbursement reconciliation;
- Part 36 consequences;
- bill/statement;
- costs negotiation/assessment;
- client deduction/payment;
- archiving.

## Product model
`ClosureReadiness`:
- damages settled;
- damages received;
- client paid;
- costs agreed/received;
- disbursements reconciled;
- experts paid;
- funding closed;
- documents complete;
- complaint open?;
- retention trigger.

## Opportunity
**Closure Automation**
- checklist;
- chase outstanding costs;
- identify unreconciled invoices;
- generate closure pack;
- trigger retention.

## KPI
- settlement-to-client-payment days;
- settlement-to-file-close days;
- unrecovered cost balance;
- files technically settled but operationally open.

## Why
Open-file tail can create hidden admin and WIP.
