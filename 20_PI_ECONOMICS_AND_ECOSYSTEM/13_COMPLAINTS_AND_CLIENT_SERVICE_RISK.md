# COMPLAINTS & CLIENT-SERVICE RISK

## Current data
Legal Ombudsman Q2 2025/26:
- poor communication = 25% of upheld complaint types;
- delay/failure to progress = 19%;
- costs = 13%;
- personal-injury complaints showed 57% poor first-tier complaint handling in investigative outcomes.

Source:
https://www.legalombudsman.org.uk/information-centre/data-centre/complaints-data/202526-quarter-2-complaints-data/

## Major product implication
Two of the biggest complaint themes map directly to our proposed platform:
- communication;
- progression/delay.

## Product opportunities

### Client Communication SLA
Track:
- last meaningful update;
- unanswered message;
- promised update date.

### Matter Progress SLA
Track:
- external wait;
- next action;
- stale matter;
- blocked reason.

### Complaint Workflow
- complaint opened;
- acknowledgement;
- owner;
- eight-week service-provider response window;
- resolution;
- Ombudsman escalation status.

Legal Ombudsman says providers must first be allowed to deal with the complaint and generally have up to eight weeks to respond.

Source:
https://www.legalombudsman.org.uk/for-consumers/factsheets/here-to-help/

## Strategic insight
Matter Command can be sold not just as labour saving but as client-service/risk infrastructure.
