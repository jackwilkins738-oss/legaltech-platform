# CASE ECONOMICS LIFECYCLE

## A matter has two timelines

### Legal/workflow timeline
intake → liability → evidence → quantum → negotiation → proceedings → settlement → costs → close.

### Economic timeline
lead/acquisition cost
→ staff work
→ disbursement spend
→ expert/medical spend
→ funding/insurance costs
→ recoverable costs stage
→ damages
→ costs recovery
→ client deduction
→ cash received
→ cash released.

## Why this matters
Two files at the same legal stage can have radically different economics.

Example:
Matter A:
- little disbursement spend;
- admitted liability;
- early settlement.

Matter B:
- £4k disbursements;
- several experts;
- disputed liability;
- 18 months old.

They should not have identical operational priority.

## Future profitability object
Per matter:
- work hours/cost;
- disbursements paid;
- disbursements committed;
- recoverable-cost stage;
- damages estimate/range;
- funding cost;
- success-fee/deduction rules;
- settlement receipts;
- outstanding costs;
- expected cash date.

## Product opportunity
`Matter Economics` dashboard:
- WIP/capacity;
- external cash tied up;
- estimated stage economics;
- stalled high-value matters;
- unrecovered disbursements.

## Boundary
Do not become accounting ledger initially.
Read/integrate financial data and calculate operational economics.
