# DEFENDANT / INSURER ECOSYSTEM

## Why model the other side
Claimant firms repeatedly interact with:
- insurer;
- TPA;
- defendant solicitor;
- compensator;
- NHS Resolution in clinical negligence;
- MIB in certain motor claims.

## Operational variables
- response SLA;
- preferred communication channel;
- portal;
- reference format;
- payment behaviour;
- offer history;
- rehabilitation handling.

## Product opportunity
`ExternalPartyProfile`:
- organisation;
- role;
- contacts;
- known reference patterns;
- response-time metrics;
- open dependencies.

## Cross-matter intelligence
"Insurer X currently averages 18 days on liability responses."

Potentially valuable, but customer data aggregation requires governance.

## Workflow
Auto-route correspondence based on:
- insurer;
- reference;
- matter;
- portal.

## Strategic benefit
External-party intelligence improves:
- matter matching;
- chasing;
- delay prediction.
