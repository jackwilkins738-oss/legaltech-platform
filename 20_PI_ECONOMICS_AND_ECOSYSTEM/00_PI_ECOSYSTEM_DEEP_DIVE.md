# UK PERSONAL INJURY ECONOMICS & ECOSYSTEM — DEEP DIVE

## Why this layer matters
A PI firm is not just fee earners + Proclaim.

Every matter interacts with:
- insurers/compensators;
- Claims Portal / OIC;
- medical experts/MROs/MedCo;
- rehabilitation providers;
- ATE insurers / funders;
- counsel;
- experts;
- courts;
- costs recovery;
- client money/damages;
- lead generators/CMCs;
- complaints/regulatory obligations.

These parties create:
- repeated admin,
- external waiting time,
- disbursement exposure,
- deadline risk,
- cash-flow risk,
- complaints,
- margin variance.

## Strategic conclusion
Our platform should eventually model **case economics and external dependency state**, not just documents and tasks.

A workflow may have low labour cost but high economic value if it:
- releases settlement earlier;
- avoids a stage/cost loss;
- speeds medical evidence;
- reduces disbursement lock-up;
- reduces complaint/PII risk.

## Core product consequences
Add future concepts:
- protocol/track/stage;
- fixed-cost stage;
- offer state;
- funding/insurance state;
- disbursement state;
- medical-report state;
- rehabilitation state;
- external-provider state;
- damages/payment state;
- complaint state.

These should be structured objects rather than notes.
