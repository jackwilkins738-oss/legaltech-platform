# REHABILITATION / TREATMENT WORKFLOW

## Protocol position
General PI Pre-Action Protocol says parties should consider rehabilitation as early as possible and throughout the protocol period.

Source:
https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil/protocol/prot_pic

## Workflow
- need identified;
- consent;
- provider referral;
- immediate-needs assessment;
- treatment recommendation;
- funding agreement;
- appointment;
- progress;
- discharge/report;
- costs/invoice.

## Product opportunity
**Rehabilitation Coordination**
- referral state;
- provider;
- appointment;
- funding;
- progress report;
- missed appointment;
- completion.

## Why it matters
May improve client outcome and case progression.

## Boundary
Do not make clinical treatment decisions.
Do not infer medical necessity autonomously.
