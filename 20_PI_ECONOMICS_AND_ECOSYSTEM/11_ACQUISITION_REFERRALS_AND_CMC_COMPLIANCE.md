# CLIENT ACQUISITION, REFERRALS & CMC COMPLIANCE

## Personal-injury referral-fee ban
LASPO prohibits payment/receipt of referral fees for prescribed personal-injury business.

SRA guidance:
https://guidance.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/prohibition-of-referral-fees-in-laspo-56-60/

## CMCs
Claims-management companies can be FCA-regulated for personal injury and related sectors.

FCA:
https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/claims-management-regulation

SRA's March 2026 claims-management guidance update emphasised:
- duplicate-instruction checks;
- informing clients where duplicate claims found;
- ensuring consent;
- not taking unfair advantage.

Source:
https://rules.sra.org.uk/news/news/sra-update-148-claims-management-guidance/

High-volume claims businesses can fail through acquisition/compliance risk even if case handling is efficient.

## Boundary
