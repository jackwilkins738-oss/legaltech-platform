# EXPERTS, COUNSEL & EXTERNAL PROFESSIONALS

## External instruction lifecycle
Typical professional:
- identify need;
- shortlist/approve;
- conflict/availability;
- send instruction;
- send bundle;
- fee quote;
- appointment/inspection;
- report/advice;
- questions/addendum;
- invoice;
- payment.

## Product opportunity
`ExternalInstruction` object.

Use for:
- medical experts;
- engineering;
- employment/accountancy;
- care/OT;
- counsel;
- costs lawyer.

## Automation
- standard instruction pack;
- missing-document check;
- chase;
- SLA;
- fee approval;
- report receipt;
- addendum tracking.

## Why valuable
These are repeated cross-firm administrative workflows with measurable delay.

## Marketplace opportunity
Later provider network / integrations.

## Conflict/independence
Expert selection must preserve independence and customer/regulatory rules.
Do not optimise solely for cheapest/fastest.
