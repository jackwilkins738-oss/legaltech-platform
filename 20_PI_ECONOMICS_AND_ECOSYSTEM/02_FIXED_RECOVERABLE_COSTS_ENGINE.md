# FIXED RECOVERABLE COSTS (FRC) — PRODUCT IMPLICATIONS

## Current CPR structure
CPR Part 45 contains fixed-cost regimes including:
- fast track;
- intermediate track;
- NIHL;
- disbursements.

The intermediate track generally covers qualifying claims up to £100,000 and has stage/complexity-band cost tables.

Practice Direction 45 provides current Tables 12 and 14.

Sources:
https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil/rules/part45-fixed-costs
https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil/rules/part45-fixed-costs/practice-direction-45-fixed-costs

## Product opportunity
A future **Cost Stage Intelligence** service can store:

- track;
- complexity band;
- current FRC stage;
- damages value;
- recoverable disbursements;
- specialist advice;
- relevant stage dates.

## What it can safely do
- detect likely stage transition;
- show applicable current cost table;
- flag missing stage data;
- calculate using deterministic configured rules;
- require professional confirmation.

## Why valuable
Case progression may have economic consequences separate from legal merits.

Operations leaders could see:
- matters near next cost stage;
- matters economically stalled;
- disbursement-heavy matters;
- settlement timing.

## Guardrail
FRC interpretation changes with rules/case law.
Version rules by:
- jurisdiction;
- effective date;
- claim type;
- track;
- complexity band.

Never let an LLM calculate authoritative recoverable costs from memory.
