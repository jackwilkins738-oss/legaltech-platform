# DISBURSEMENT CAPITAL & CASH FLOW

## Hidden economics
PI firms may pay/commit:
- medical reports;
- records;
- expert fees;
- counsel;
- court fees;
- searches;
- rehab/assessments.

Cash may be tied up long before settlement/costs recovery.

## Product opportunity
A **Disbursement Exposure** layer:
- requested;
- approved;
- committed;
- invoiced;
- paid;
- recoverable?;
- recovered;
- written off.

## Operations questions
- Which provider has unpaid invoices?
- Which matters have high cash exposure?
- Which old matters have unrecovered disbursements?
- Which stage causes most capital lock-up?
- Are funded disbursements within facility limits?

## Strategic implication
A service that shortens case cycle can improve cash conversion even if labour savings are modest.

## Pilot ROI
Measure:
- days from disbursement to recovery;
- average outstanding disbursement/matter;
- total capital tied up;
- provider turnaround.
