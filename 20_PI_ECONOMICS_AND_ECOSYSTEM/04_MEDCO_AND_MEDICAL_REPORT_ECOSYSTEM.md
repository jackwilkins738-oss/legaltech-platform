# MEDCO & MEDICAL REPORTING ECOSYSTEM

## Current MedCo mechanics
For relevant RTA soft-tissue/whiplash claims:
- instructing parties register with MedCo;
- experts must be accredited;
- MROs are audited;
- MedCo search returns a random/fair shortlist ('Offer');
- direct financial links are declared;
- report itself is not uploaded to MedCo; anonymised case data is.

Source:
https://medco.org.uk/faq-items/how-does-medco-work/

Experts must remain accredited and meet CPD requirements.

Source:
https://medco.org.uk/medical-expert-cpd-hours-2026-to-2027-information-for-auts-and-mros/

MedCo has announced revised RTA3 fields effective 4 January 2027.

Source:
https://medco.org.uk/advance-notice-of-updated-rta3-medical-report/

## Workflow state
`MedicalInstruction`:
- requires MedCo?
- MedCo case ID;
- offer generated;
- provider selected;
- appointment booked;
- attended;
- report awaited;
- report received;
- factual accuracy review;
- clarification/addendum;
- disclosed;
- invoiced;
- paid.

## Product opportunity
**Medical Evidence Operations**
- manage MedCo state;
- chase appointment/report;
- detect no-show;
- factual-accuracy review task;
- extract prognosis;
- detect recommended further expert;
- link disbursement.

## Strategic point
Do not become an MRO accidentally.

A software/administrative platform must not cross into functions that make it an unauthorised medical-reporting organisation.

Any deeper service needs specialist legal/MedCo review.
