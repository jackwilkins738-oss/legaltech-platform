# PII / CYBER / RISK ECONOMICS

## Law-firm PII
Current Law Society guidance says underwriting depends on factors including:
- turnover;
- partners;
- volume/type of work.

Source:
https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/professional-indemnity-insurance/renewing-your-firms-pii

## Cyber
Law Society warned in late 2025 that increasing AI adoption and cyber threats could harden cyber-insurance conditions.

Source:
https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/Topics/Cybersecurity/Partner-content/Understanding-cyber-insurance-for-law-firms

## Product implication
Long term, platform evidence can help firms demonstrate:
- supervision;
- audit;
- deadline control;
- access control;
- incident handling;
- workflow consistency.

We should not promise insurance-premium reductions.

## Commercial angle
Risk-control evidence can be part of renewal/procurement conversations:
- audit exports;
- access reports;
- exception metrics;
- incident logs;
- approval records.

## Opportunity
Insurer/broker partnerships later, only after mature evidence.
