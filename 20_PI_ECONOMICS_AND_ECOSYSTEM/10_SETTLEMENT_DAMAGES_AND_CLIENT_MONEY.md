# SETTLEMENT, DAMAGES & CLIENT-MONEY WORKFLOW

## Matter operations
After settlement:
- settlement terms;
- compensation received;
- costs/disbursements;
- funding/ATE;
- client deductions;
- client account;
- statement;
- payment to client;
- residual costs;
- closure.

## Regulatory risk
SRA has taken action in PI matters over improper damages deductions.

The SRA also issued a 2026 warning on high-volume 'no win, no fee' practices and transparency.

Sources:
https://www.sra.org.uk/no-win-no-fee
https://rules.sra.org.uk/news/news/releases/nwnf-warning/

## Product opportunity
**Settlement Completion Checklist**
- settlement amount;
- authority;
- funds received;
- deductions supported by agreement;
- client statement;
- approval;
- payment;
- costs outstanding.

## Boundary
Do not hold client money.
Do not become legal accounting engine in V1.

Integrate with accounting and ensure workflow approvals.

## Strategic value
Settlement completion can be operationally repetitive and client-sensitive.
TO should measure it.
