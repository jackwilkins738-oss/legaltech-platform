# REVISED LONG-TERM PRODUCT VISION

## Old simplification
Email → classify → file → task.

## Deeper model
A legal matter is a network of internal and external commitments.

The platform should continuously understand:

### Internal
- tasks;
- evidence;
- deadlines;
- approvals;
- client communications.

### External
- insurer;
- medical provider;
- expert;
- rehabilitation;
- court;
- funder;
- portal.

### Economic
- staff capacity;
- disbursements;
- FRC stage;
- offers;
- settlement cash;
- costs recovery.

## Matter Command 2.0
The core screen should eventually answer:

1. What is this matter waiting for?
2. Who is responsible?
3. When should it have happened?
4. What evidence/document proves that?
5. What is the client/risk/economic consequence?
6. Can the system safely progress it?
7. If not, which human needs to decide?

## Strategic differentiation
This is much deeper than:
- PMS task list;
- AI chat;
- email filing.

It becomes a **matter progression operating system**.

## Strong long-term position
**We don't just store the matter. We understand what is blocking it from moving forward.**
