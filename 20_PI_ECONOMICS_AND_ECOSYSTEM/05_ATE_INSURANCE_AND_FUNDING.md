# ATE INSURANCE, LEGAL-EXPENSE COVER & FUNDING

## Funding options matter operationally
Claimants may have:
- before-the-event legal expenses;
- trade-union cover;
- CFA/DBA;
- after-the-event insurance;
- third-party funding in some cases.

GOV.UK:
https://www.gov.uk/compensation-after-accident-or-injury/check-your-insurance-policies

Legal Ombudsman PI material also discusses ATE and Part 36/cost risk:
https://www.legalombudsman.org.uk/for-consumers/complaints-process/using-a-legal-service-provider/personal-injury-complaints/

## SRA 2026 funding scrutiny
SRA issued new third-party litigation funding guidance July/August 2026 focused on:
- client best interests;
- independence;
- conflicts;
- informed decisions;
- financial crime;
- firm stability.

Source:
https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/using-arranging-third-party-litigation-funding/
