# CFA / DBA / 'NO WIN NO FEE' ONBOARDING

## Current SRA focus
On 28 January 2026 SRA issued a formal warning focused on high-volume consumer claims and 'no win, no fee' arrangements.

Concerns include:
- unclear fees/risks;
- commercial interests overriding client interests;
- poor transparency.

Source:
https://consultations.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/no-win-no-fee-high-volume-consumer-claims/

## Product opportunity
**Funding & Cost Disclosure Workflow**
Before instruction completes:
- agreement type;
- funding alternatives considered;
- success fee/deduction explanation;
- disbursement risk;
- ATE;
- cancellation/termination consequences;
- signed acknowledgement;
- accessibility/understanding prompts.

## Evidence
Store:
- version of terms;
- key-facts document;
- date shown;
- acknowledgement;
- adviser/reviewer.

## Benefit
Reduces onboarding variability, complaints and regulatory exposure.

## Boundary
Content must be firm/counsel approved.
Software does not provide legal advice to claimant.
