# EXTERNAL VALIDATION PLAN

## Objective
Determine whether the partner branch is representative and whether independent buyers will pay.

## Minimum sample
5 firms before committing heavily.
10 is better.

Target mix:
- 3+ current Proclaim PI/claims firms
- 1 strong/satisfied Proclaim user
- 1 firm considering Evo/replacement
- 1 adjacent volume-litigation firm if possible

---

# Interview roles

At each firm ideally speak to:
1. operations leader
2. frontline user/team leader
3. IT/applications where possible

---

# Interview structure

## Part A — firm/environment
- headcount
- practice areas
- Proclaim users
- Outlook/M365
- internal Proclaim resource

## Part B — workflow
- what arrives each day?
- how is it filed?
- how are tasks created?
- what gets chased?
- where is work copied/rekeyed?
- what happens outside Proclaim?

## Part C — pain economics
- where do backlogs form?
- which admin role is growing?
- which work causes overtime/hiring?
- how many people touch the process?

## Part D — change
- who can modify Proclaim?
- time/cost to change workflow?
- support dependency?
- Evo plans?

## Part E — automation trust
Would they allow:
- read-only mailbox access?
- selected shared mailbox?
- Proclaim API?
- AI processing?
- auto-filing?
- internal task creation?
- draft client emails?

Identify boundary, not just "yes/no."

## Part F — commercial
"If this removed X hours/month with audit and human controls, who would own the budget?"

Then:
"What would you need to see before paying for a pilot?"

Avoid prematurely forcing a price.

---

# Validation criteria

## Problem validated
At least 3 independent firms report the same workflow pain with meaningful volume.

## Buyer validated
At least 2 identify a credible budget owner.

## Access validated
At least 2 say controlled technical access is plausible.

## Pilot validated
At least 1 is willing to discuss a paid pilot after proof.

---

# Disconfirming evidence we actively want

Find firms where:
- Proclaim works extremely well;
- internal team already automated it;
- Evo removes the pain;
- workflow isn't expensive;
- security would block access;
- staff prefer current method.

Those interviews are especially valuable.
