# TARGET ACCOUNT VALIDATION CAMPAIGN

## Objective
Use current public evidence to prioritise external validation rather than randomly contacting law firms.

## Tier 1 — strongest combination of Proclaim + claims operations

### 1. Minster Law
Evidence:
- current careers taxonomy includes Lead Proclaim Developer and Proclaim Developer;
- current roles include OIC, MOJ, fast-track, EL/PL, portal, operations and serious injury;
- firm describes some of the UK's largest legal teams.

Why:
large operational claims environment + internal IT/Proclaim capability.

Hypothesis to test:
Where does Proclaim still require human coordination despite strong internal technical capability?

### 2. Ralli Ltd
Evidence:
- current first-party careers material explicitly says it operates Proclaim CMS / paperless Proclaim;
- current PI roles cover RTA, EL/PL and multi-track work;
- 40+ staff stated on careers site.

Why:
direct current Proclaim + specialist PI.

### 3. Express Solicitors
Evidence:
- current public staff page identifies a Proclaim Developer;
- large claimant-focused practice.

Why:
good test of whether firms with in-house Proclaim expertise still have operations pain.

### 4. Fletchers Group
Evidence:
current/recent clinical-negligence/PI roles have referenced Proclaim.

Why:
large PI/clinical-negligence operational environment.

### 5. Robert James Solicitors
Evidence:
current/recent PI/RTA careers material references Proclaim and high caseloads.

Why:
high-volume claimant workflow.

## Interview mix
Do not only interview unhappy Proclaim users.

Minimum:
- 3 firms with obvious volume pain;
- 1 firm with strong internal Proclaim capability;
- 1 satisfied/defensive Proclaim user;
- 1 firm considering Evo/replacement if available.

## Roles to target
Priority:
1. COO / Operations Director
2. Head of Claims / PI
3. Applications / Proclaim Manager
4. IT Director
5. Team Leader
6. Managing Partner / CEO where appropriate

## Questions the campaign must answer
- What is still manual?
- What is already automated successfully?
- What requires specialist Proclaim resource?
- What takes longest to change?
- What happens in Outlook/Excel outside Proclaim?
- What external dependency causes most delay?
- Would controlled Graph/API access be acceptable?
- Who owns budget?
- What result would justify a paid pilot?
