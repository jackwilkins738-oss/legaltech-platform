# PILOT ECONOMICS — SENSITIVITY

## Formula
Annual capacity value =
users × events/day × minutes saved/event × working days ÷ 60 × loaded hourly cost.

## Example scenarios at £25/hour, 220 days

### Small team
15 users × 8 events × 2 min saved
= 880 hours/year
= £22,000 capacity value.

Weak basis for £40k ACV.

### Mid team
30 users × 15 events × 2.5 min
= 4,125 hours
= £103,125 capacity value.

Could support a meaningful department contract if realised.

### Large workflow
75 users × 15 events × 3 min
= 12,375 hours
= £309,375 capacity value.

Can support enterprise pricing if adoption/accuracy are real.

## Realisation discount
Always run:
- 25%
- 50%
- 75%
of theoretical saving.

A workflow is attractive if economics still work at 50% realisation.

## Additional value
Model separately:
- avoided hire;
- cycle-time reduction;
- disbursement cash release;
- fewer complaints;
- increased matters/FTE.

Do not add risk-value numbers without evidence.

## Pilot price decision
Provisional:
£5k–£15k remains reasonable for early paid validation if expected annual value is materially larger.

Do not lock annual ACV until customer evidence.
