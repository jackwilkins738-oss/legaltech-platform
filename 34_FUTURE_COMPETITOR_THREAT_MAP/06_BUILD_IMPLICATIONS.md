# Build Implications

## Becoming commodity
Summarisation, email classification, basic extraction, simple drafting, generic task creation, generic workflow builders and standalone chat/RAG.

## Potential durable value
Legacy-system interoperability; high-confidence matter matching; exception-first operations; external-dependency progression; UK practice-specific state machines; auditable human approval; implementation/migration assets; cross-matter ops intelligence; accumulated correction/eval data.

## Buyer test
A buyer must be able to answer: **Why buy this instead of waiting for Proclaim, Microsoft, Clio, Filevine or another incumbent?**

The answer cannot be “our AI is better.” It should become: **we can improve this exact workflow in your current estate, prove the economics, deploy safely without replacing your PMS, and carry the workflow/mappings forward if you migrate later.**
