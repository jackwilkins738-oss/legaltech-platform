# Research Protocol

For every CRITICAL/HIGH competitor track current ICP/geography, system of record, unique data asset, workflow/action capability, human-review controls, integrations, pricing where public, UK/PI depth, migration capability, published outcomes, acquisitions/funding and likely next adjacency.

Evidence labels: VERIFIED CURRENT, VERIFIED HISTORICAL, COMPANY CLAIM, THIRD-PARTY REPORT, INFERENCE, UNKNOWN. Never convert vendor marketing into measured market facts.
