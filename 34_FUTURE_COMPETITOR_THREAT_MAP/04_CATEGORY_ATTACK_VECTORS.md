# Category Attack Vectors

1. **PMS/CMS incumbents** — embed AI where matter state already lives.
2. **AI-native legal platforms** — expand from research/drafting into operational actions.
3. **PI/plaintiff specialists** — win through vertical data and case-outcome context.
4. **Microsoft/horizontal agent platforms** — commoditise generic orchestration.
5. **DMS/knowledge incumbents** — exploit privileged document context.
6. **Legal-data giants** — combine authoritative law with agents/workflow builders.
7. **Internal law-firm teams** — build on Microsoft/APIs/LLMs instead of buying.

Defence: own a measurable workflow, legacy interoperability, exception handling, UK-specific state machines, audit, deployment/migration assets and operational telemetry.
