# Quarterly Competitive Watch

Escalate a competitor one tier immediately if any of these occur:

1. UK law-firm launch into the 50–250 user segment.
2. Proclaim connector, migration tooling or Access partnership.
3. Native Outlook/Graph ingestion tied to matter progression.
4. Agent performs external actions and writes results back to matter state.
5. PI/clinical workflow pack covering records, experts, offers, deadlines or settlements.
6. OIC, Claims Portal, MedCo, HMCTS or UK insurer integration.
7. Acquisition of UK PMS/workflow consultancy/Proclaim specialist.
8. Published customer result showing >20% case-cycle reduction or >1 FTE-equivalent capacity per team.
9. Pricing makes a standalone augmentation layer hard to justify.
10. Major UK Proclaim customer migrates for AI/workflow capability.

Cadence: CRITICAL monthly; HIGH quarterly; MEDIUM six-monthly; WATCH annually unless a trigger fires.
