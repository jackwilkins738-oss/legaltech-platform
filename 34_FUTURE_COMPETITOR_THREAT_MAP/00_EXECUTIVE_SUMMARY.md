# Future Competitor Threat Map — 2026 to 2031

Total mapped: **40 companies/platforms**.

## Core conclusion
The market is converging toward the same thesis: **systems of record are becoming systems of action**.

The most dangerous competitors combine privileged matter/document context, workflow/action capability, incumbent distribution, domain-specific legal data, governed human approval and system-of-record writeback.

## Highest-threat group
**9 CRITICAL** threats: Filevine / LOIS, Litify / ACE, EvenUp, Eve / EveOS, Microsoft Copilot Studio + Power Platform, Clio + vLex / Vincent, Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, LexisNexis Protege, NetDocuments ndMAX.

## Strategic implication
Do not attempt to win on generic email classification, summarisation, extraction, drafting or task creation. Those capabilities are rapidly commoditising. Differentiation must come from workflow depth, legacy interoperability, exceptions/auditability, UK legal-process depth, deployment/migration speed and accumulated operational telemetry.

Probability and damage scores are strategic heuristics, not statistical forecasts.
