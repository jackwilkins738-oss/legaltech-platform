# Commercial rules
- Capacity is not cash saving unless a realisation mechanism exists.
- Pricing remains hypothesis until buyer/pilot evidence.
- One enthusiastic interview is not market validation.
- Preserve buyer, user, IT/security and procurement objections separately.
